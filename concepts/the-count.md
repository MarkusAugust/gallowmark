## The number

In [[the-counted-lands]] every child is numbered at birth, the number pricked into the inside of
the wrist. The number decides what work you do, whom you may marry, and when your number comes up.
When it does, you are taken, quietly, in the night, and your house is given to the next number.

## The happiness

Nobody starves in the Counted Lands. Nobody is sick, nobody is at war, nobody is afraid of anything
but their number, and most are not afraid of that. They say the [[ossar]] care for them. The
[[the-numbered-man]] said that was the worst of it.
