## The day

At dawn on Ashday every household in [[gallowmark]] takes a handful of old ash and marks a cross on
each forehead. Nobody speaks until noon. At noon someone says the names of the dead of the grey,
as many as anyone remembers, and then the drinking starts, and lasts.

## Custom

The Ashborn do not mark their foreheads. They say they carry enough.
