## The Mark

Ash-bread, baked dark and heavy from rye grown in ash-soil; turnips, mutton, sour ale. On
[[ashday]], nothing until noon.

## The Rimewold

Smoked fish, seal fat, horse when a horse dies, and blood-mead, thick and red, drunk from horns at
[[the-icething]].

## Crownhold

Wheat bread, wine, mulled with spice at court and with nothing in the provinces. Peacock at
banquets, and poison at the same banquets, so the court eats slowly.

## Harrowmere

Eel pie from the black lake, pickled herring, gin. The princes eat plainly and drink nothing.

## The Glassands

Dates, goat, flatbread baked on hot stone, and qasr, a clear spirit pressed from the glass-cactus,
that the [[the-hadd]] drink only at dawn when the walking is over.

## The Feverfen

Fish, reed-root, and fever-bark tea from [[the-leech-mothers]], bitter enough to stop the shakes.
