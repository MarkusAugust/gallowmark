## The feast

On the longest day of the year the domes of [[crownhold]] are uncovered to the sun, the Exarch
[[hadrian-mournevale]] crowns the king anew in light, and every temple carries its tithe in
procession to the capital. It is the richest day of the church's year by a wide margin.
