## The oath

Put your hand on a hangman's rope, say what you will do, and the [[the-ninth-rope]] will tie a
knot in it and keep it. That is a rope-oath. It is the oldest law in Harth and the only one
nobody can buy out of, not even in [[harrowmere]].

## Breaking it

The oath-breaker feels the rope at once: a tightness at the throat that does not go away. Each day
it is worse. Breath shortens, the face darkens, the voice goes. Keep the oath, late, and it
loosens. Do not, and in a season or a year the rope finishes what it began.

## Custom

Kings swear rope-oaths at their crowning, captains at their hiring, reavers on [[the-teeth]] by
the Captain's Oath, which is the same thing with a mast for a gallows.
