## What they are

The first coin of every pact is the body, and the body keeps the receipt. A small asking leaves
a small mark: a vein gone black under the wrist, a nail turned to horn. A great one leaves a
great one: skin that is glass in patches, like [[qeth-ummar]]'s; a voice that comes from slightly
behind the mouth; an eye that no longer blinks or weeps.

## Reading them

Physicians, [[the-leech-mothers]] and [[the-ninth-rope]] can read pactmarks the way a clerk reads
a ledger: how many askings, how large, and how close the second coin is. Sorcerers cover them
with gloves, veils and paint. [[mardra]] does not, and her marks are fewer than they should be.
