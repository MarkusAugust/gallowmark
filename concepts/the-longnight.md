## In the north

The [[rimehounds]] come south out of the ice on the Longnight, and what is outside a wall is
theirs. The clans bring in the herds, bar the doors, and sing as loud as they can until dawn. In
[[bonereach]] and lately in the rest of the Mark, they do the same.

## In the south

The [[the-kneeling]] hold that the Longnight is when the dark below is nearest, and kneel all
night, every cell in Harth at once. The Sunward call it the night of the enemy. The hangmen hunt.
