## Gallowmark

- *By the Ashfall.* The oath of the Ashborn, never by a god.
- *The rope remembers.* A debt is never forgotten.
- *What do you want?* The question the Reach asks instead of where you have been.

## The free companies

- *She has him.* Said of the dead, for [[nevermourn]].
- *Weep later, and pay the fine.*

## Crownhold

- *Under the gold, the iron.* Everything fine hides something hard.
- *Helion sees.* Said piously, and as a threat.

## Harrowmere

- *Clasped hands, open purse.*
- *Everyone owes. The question is whom.*

## The Glassands

- *Walk at night, drink at dawn.*
- *Sun-fool.* The worst thing a Hadd can call you.

## The Rimewold

- *Behind me stand —* followed by a number. A boast, and an argument.
- *Bar the door.* Goodbye, on the Longnight.
