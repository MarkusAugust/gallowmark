## The letters

A schuldbrief is a debt written down: who owes, whom, how much, by when, sealed by a Harrowmere
house and sworn with a hand on a hangman's rope. Once written it can be sold, and most are, many
times. The debtor owes whoever holds the paper. Most debtors do not know who that is.

## Inheriting

Debts pass to heirs in Harth, and so do schuldbrieven. A child can be born owing. [[stillwater]]
is full of people who never signed what put them there.

## The rope

Because they are sworn as [[rope-oaths]], a schuldbrief that goes unpaid tightens. The hangmen
say every one paid pulls the knot of [[the-first-knot]] a little tighter, which is why
[[the-ninth-rope]] defends Harrowmere's debts and despises Harrowmere. The [[the-burning-of-the-rolls]]
burns schuldbrieven, and the hangmen are afraid of what that does.
