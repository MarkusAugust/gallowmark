## The night

On the anniversary the hangmen keep for [[the-first-knot]], [[the-ninth-rope]] climbs all nine
gallows of [[hangmoot]] and tightens every knot by hand. In the Mark, every village with a gallows
does the same, and then drinks under it until dawn. Debts are paid on Knotnight, oaths sworn, and
the last unpaid debt of the year is hanged.

## Elsewhere

In [[crownhold]] and [[harrowmere]] Knotnight is a night of masks and rope-games whose meaning nobody
remembers. [[the-kneeling]] remember. They cut a rope somewhere every Knotnight.
