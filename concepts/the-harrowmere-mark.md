## The coin

A thin silver disc, a ship on the face, two hands clasped on the back, and the mint-mark of the
House of Salt on the rim. Every kingdom of Harth strikes its own coin. Every kingdom of Harth
prices in marks.

## The trust

Nobody trusts it. Each house of [[the-gilded]] shaves the silver a little when it suits them, and
the weight of a mark has fallen every generation since [[the-fall-of-vahr]]. People take it
anyway, because the alternative is to be paid in something worse, and in Harth the something worse
is [[schuldbrieven]].

## Sayings

- *Clasped hands, open purse.*
- *A mark is a promise from a liar, and the liar is rich.*
