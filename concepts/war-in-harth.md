## Free companies

No king of Harth can afford an army. They keep a guard and buy the rest: free companies like
[[the-unmourned]], hired at [[fordham]], [[harrowmere]] or the camp-fairs of [[crownhold]], paid
in [[the-harrowmere-mark]] or in plunder. A company fights for its contract, swears a
[[rope-oaths]] on it, and worships [[nevermourn]].

## Gates and walls

Harth is a land of walls: Vahrish fortresses, river cities, the holdfasts of the Mark. Battles are
rare. Sieges are not. Whoever holds the gates holds the land, which is why every side wants the
[[greycloaks]] and nobody trusts them.

## Sorcerers in war

A sorcerer bought at [[the-hiring-of-the-wise]] can sour a well, send a fever, turn a river, make
an army forget its orders. Every asking costs the sorcerer, and it costs the land: a field
poisoned by sorcery stays poisoned. Captains hire them anyway, and hang them after if they can.

## Horse

Crownhold's armoured knights are the heaviest horse in Harth and were built for one enemy: the
[[the-hoofborn]] of [[the-grass-sea]], who fight from the saddle with the bow and never charge.
Sixty years ago the knights lost.
