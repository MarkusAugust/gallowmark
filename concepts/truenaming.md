## The art

The [[river-priests]] hold that every thing has a true name, under the one people call it, and
that the true name is a handle. Say it, and you have hold. A flood named truly goes down a
finger, as the river went down under [[vell]]'s feet. A man named truly does what he was going
to do anyway, but cannot stop.

## The cost

Truenaming is not a pact. Nothing below is asked. The price is paid in the namer's own name: each
true name given takes a little of the giver's. Young priests have long names. Brother [[ruun]] is
called by one syllable, and some days he does not answer to it.
