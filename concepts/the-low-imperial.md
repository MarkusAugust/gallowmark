## The tongue

When [[old-vahr]] fell its language broke like everything else, but its roads kept a version of it
alive: the Low Imperial, the speech of milestones, markets and armies. It is the tongue of trade in
[[harrowmere]], of law in [[crownhold]], and of every free company.

## The others

Every land keeps its own tongue beside it: the hard northern speech of the Mark and the Rimewold,
the courtly Low Imperial of Crownhold, the merchant-speech of Harrowmere, the Hadd tongue of the
glass, the water-speech of the fen. The High Imperial survives only in temples and in
[[the-hungry-books]].
