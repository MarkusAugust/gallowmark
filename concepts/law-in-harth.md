## The Mark

The [[the-ninth-rope]] and the Reeve's moot at [[hangmoot]]: what can be paid is paid, what cannot
is hanged or forgiven. The [[greycloaks]] tithe in between, and call it law.

## Crownhold

The king's courts, for those who can pay the judges. For those who cannot, the magistrates. For
nobles, the poison-cup, offered politely and drunk the same way.

## Harrowmere

Everything is a debt. A crime is a debt to the injured, written as a [[schuldbrieven]] and sold.
Those who cannot pay go to [[stillwater]].

## The Glassands

Blood is paid in water, weighed against the dead, as in [[ush-kaddah]]. Outside the city, a
[[the-well-kings]] judges, and the judgment is usually thirst.

## The Teeth

The Captain's Oath, and the mast.
