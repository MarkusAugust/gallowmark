## Who

Lucius is forty, enormous, painted, and always eating. He laughs at everything his Harrowmere
councillors say and signs whatever they put in front of him, as his line always has. He also reads
what he signs, at night, alone, and keeps copies. He owns more of his own kingdom than the House of
Salt believes, through names they have never thought to check.

## Voice

Jolly, wet, slow. He lets people finish their sentences, and remembers them.
