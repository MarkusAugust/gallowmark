## Who

Seraphine is nineteen, thin, quiet, and the only one of the king's children who prays. The
court thinks she prays to the gods of the chapel. She prays with her forehead on the cellar
floor, and something answers her.

## What she wants

The iron circlet her father wears under the gold. [[the-kneeling]] have told her it is one of
the rings of [[the-first-knot]], carried south out of [[old-vahr]], and that a ring broken in
the right place loosens the rope. She has not told them she would rather wear it.
