## Who

Quintus was a chapel priest in a grain province, until the year the [[greycloaks]] took the
tithe in seed-corn and the province starved. He put his right arm into the chapel fire and
held it there while the village watched, and said a hand that had blessed the tithe would
bless nothing more. He has been walking since. The stump is black. He does not cover it.

## Voice

He speaks in fire: short, rising, and the crowd says the last word with him.
