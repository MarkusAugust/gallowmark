## Who

A Kneeler so long in the pits that the rats sleep on her. She kneels in the deepest pit with her
hands in the ash and the young rats come up between her fingers. She does not fight. She calls.

## What she does

She raises [[knotrats]] by the thousand for the [[the-kneeling]], to chew the ropes of
[[hangmoot]] from underneath. When she is pressed, she sets the old pits burning.
