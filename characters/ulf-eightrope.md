## Who

Ulf is huge, slow, sixty, and was hanged at fifteen like every hangman. When he became Reeve he took
the name *Eightrope* in front of the moot, and swore a [[rope-oaths]] that he would not be the Reeve
who counted seven. The scar at his throat is the oath's.

## What he does

He holds [[hangmoot]] with eight ropes and an empty ninth gallows. He sends hangmen to [[the-brink]],
to [[solmerine]] after [[the-kneeling]], and to every holdfast in the Mark that has forgotten its
debts. He has begun, like [[haldis-graymane]] before him, to watch the [[greycloaks]].
