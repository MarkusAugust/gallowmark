## The story

On the night [[old-vahr]] burned, a gate on the eastern road was the only way out. Marhaun, a
knight of the emperor's guard, held it: not against the fire, against the people in the city who
were already changing. For three days he let the living out and kept the rest in, and when the
last of the living had gone through he shut the gate behind them and stayed on the inside.

## Now

The gate stands in the [[glassands]], half melted, still shut. The [[the-hadd]] say a figure in
armour stands behind it on still nights. The [[the-tenth-legion]] were supposed to hold that gate.
They had left it open.
