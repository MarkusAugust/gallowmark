## Who

Nobody knows how old Oulu-Nae is. She was old when she taught [[mardra]]. She is small, folded,
black to the shoulders with leech-stain, and her eyes are gone: she says she left them inside
[[the-black-step]], one at a time, as the price of looking.

## What she knows

As much as Mardra, and older. She does not sell it. She will tell it to a Leech-Mother on her last
lesson, and to nobody else.
