## Who

Harl was a young man when the ash came down hot, and he pulled his sister out of a burning house
with his right hand. The hand is grey now, to the wrist, hard as horn, and does not feel. He is
seventy and has led the Ashen for forty years.

## Voice

Slow. He asks a question, then waits for the whole answer, then waits longer. People tell him more
than they meant to.
