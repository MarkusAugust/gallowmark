## Who

Saelith arrives with the first white sail every summer and leaves with the last. She takes the same
rooms on the Grand Canal of [[harrowmere]], pays in silver that does not tarnish, and is gracious to
everyone. Merchants who dealt with her as young men deal with her as old ones, and she looks the
same.

## What she buys

[[ossar]] bones, in quantity, from [[qarr-dhuun]]. Iron, salt, maps. She asks every prince of
[[the-gilded]] about [[the-nine-irons]], and has asked [[aleid-van-der-haalst]] seven times.
