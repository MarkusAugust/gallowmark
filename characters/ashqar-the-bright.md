## Who

Ashqar took his first well at nineteen and his fourth at twenty-five. He is beautiful, and half of
him is glass: the left side of his face, his left hand, a band across his chest, all clear as water
and lit faintly from inside. He has paid the body and the memory. He does not remember his mother.

## What he wants

The throne of [[old-vahr]], and what [[qeth-ummar]] reads every night on [[the-last-asking]].
