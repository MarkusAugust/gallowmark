## Who

Nask walks the banks. Barefoot, wet to the knee, unwelcome at most tables and
untroubled by it. Of all the [[river-priests]], Nask is the one villages send
for when a thing needs a name and nobody wants to be the one who gave it.

## Voice

Ask Nask a question and you get a name. Ask why and you get the name again,
slower. The priests hold that to name a thing truly is to have a grip on it,
and Nask has never been heard to let go.

## In the world

- The river-priests named [[linelord]], and it was Nask who said it first, at a
  ford in [[bonereach]], to a man who had asked what the thing in his hands
  was for. Then Nask walked on.
- Nask has never named [[sarn]] aloud. Asked, Nask names the river instead.
