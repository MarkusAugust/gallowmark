## What the Gilded bought

When [[the-gilded]] bought the debts of [[old-vahr]], one of the debts came with a house. Under the
black lake of [[harrowmere]] a [[the-stonesleepers]] lay in a niche older than the city, and the
emperors had been paying it to sleep. The princes kept paying: a chest a year, on the night of
[[the-hiring-of-the-wise]], to [[the-tenth-palace]]. It slept.

## The door

The night of [[the-fraying]] it woke. In the morning the Tenth Palace had a door. A year later the
Tenth Prince sat in the council of the Gilded for the first time: tall, pale, cold, polite, wearing
a mask of silver because the princes asked it to. It has sat there since. It votes. It lends. It
is paid more than ever, and the princes no longer say what for.
