## The title

The Linelord is not a man. It is a title, and the [[linelord]] gives it: whoever the
ledger names as holding the most ground in [[gallowmark]], wall by wall and day by day,
is the Linelord until someone holds more. The title has changed hands more often than the
crown of [[kell]] did, and nobody has kept it by deserving it. They kept it by holding.

## Jarn

Jarn holds it now. He came down to [[wrackhead]] the first winter of [[the-ebb]] with an
iron blade that came up out of Kell, and he has not gone further. He does not need to. He
holds the shingle, and the ledger counts it, and every low water he walks the length of
the beach dragging the blade behind him, and leaves a line in the wet sand from the fort to
the salt-pile.

The line says: this far is mine. The flood takes it twice a day. He draws it again twice a
day. That is the whole of what he does, and it is enough to hold the title.

## Voice

Jarn does not argue. He points at the line. If you cross it, he asks whether you meant to.

## In the world

- The pickers going down the [[quaysteps]] step over his line, and he lets them; what is
  under the water is not the shingle.
- [[tolm]] tithes everyone on the coast and has never asked Jarn for a tenth. The fort
  keeps its own line, on the other side of his.
- Brother [[ruun]] writes names in the same sand. Jarn has never drawn across one.
