## Who

Hollin went down the [[quaysteps]] the first winter of [[the-ebb]], when the top step had
shown for three days and nobody else would put a foot on it. She came up with a bollard-ring
and a cough, and went down again the next low water. The [[pickers]] came after her. They
still come after her. That is the whole of her claim, and the ledger has counted it every day
since: she was first, so the steps are hers.

She is old now. She counts the steps every morning, top to bottom, and the count is always
the same, and she is always pleased by it.

## Voice

Hollin talks about who came first. She does not talk about what came after.

## The hold

The ledger calls whoever holds the most of the Quaysteps the **Holder of the Quaysteps**.
It has called her that for longer than anyone has held anything else in [[kell]]. It does not
say she has held them well. It says she has held them.
