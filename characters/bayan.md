## Who

Bayan is twenty-six, handsome, generous, and adored by the young riders of [[the-saddle-throne]]. He
does not care about the [[ossar]]. He cares about [[solmerine]], its gold domes, its sun-sea, and the
fat king who sits in it. He shares the throne with [[sartaq]] and would rather not.
