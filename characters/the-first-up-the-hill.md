## The night

Somebody went first. The coast has argued for forty years about who, and nobody has a name.
Whoever it was got further than the others: to the top of the lane, to the first house with a
bar, and put a fist to the door, and the sea came in through the windows of the house before
the door gave.

## Now

At the top of the deepest lane of the [[lowstreets]] there is a door that has been knocked on for
forty years. The knocking stops when somebody comes up the lane behind. It turns round, and
calls the street to it, and asks you to open the door.
