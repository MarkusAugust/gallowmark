## The story

He was a digger of [[qarr-dhuun]] who lost his way, and walked south instead of north, for forty
nights, across the glass. He came back eleven years later out of the south, thin, quiet, with a
number tattooed on the inside of his wrist, and walked into [[ush-kaddah]] and drank from the well
for a whole day.

## After

He would not say what was in [[the-counted-lands]]. He would not sleep under a roof. He said, once,
drunk, that the people there were happy, and that this was the worst thing about it.
