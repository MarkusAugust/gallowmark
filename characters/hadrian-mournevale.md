## Who

Hadrian Mournevale is sixty, tall, beautifully spoken, and wears tinted spectacles that the court
believes are for weak eyes. They are for silver ones. The Mournevales are one of the oldest
houses of [[crownhold]] and, secretly, one of the [[argentine-houses]].

## His game

He has crowned no heir in [[the-three-crowns]] and courted all three. He preaches the sun against
the dark below, and he means it in his way: he believes the dark is coming up whatever anyone
does, and that the throne should be held, when it does, by someone who is related to it.
