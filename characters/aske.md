## Who

Aske rode into [[hookford]] alone eleven years ago, found the gate the bridge-folk had left open for the carts, and sat down in it. He has not left the bridge since. He is grey at forty, soft-voiced, and keeps his cloak brushed.

## What he does

He takes the tenth of the [[the-greywood]] for the tithe-house fire and the books, writes down who paid, and thanks them. Nobody in Hookford has heard him raise his voice. Nobody in Hookford has refused him twice.
