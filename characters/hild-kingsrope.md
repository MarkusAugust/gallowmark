## The story

A king of [[crownhold]] in the Broken Years swore a [[rope-oaths]] to pay a debt to the Mark and did
not. Hild, a hangman of [[the-ninth-rope]], walked south alone, took him out of his own hall at
night, carried him north across his own kingdom, and hanged him on the ninth gallows of
[[hangmoot]] in front of the moot. The kingdom did not go to war. The next king paid.

## The rope

Her rope hangs on Hangmoot still, apart from the others, and nobody has used it since. The Reeve
[[haldis-graymane]] has started looking at it.
