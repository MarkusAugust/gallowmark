## What

Nobody agrees on whether Sarn is a woman, a man, or a thing that has decided it is a person.
The stories agree on three points: Sarn has no face that anyone remembers a minute after
seeing it, Sarn has yellow eyes that they remember for the rest of their lives, and Sarn
took the three rubies out of the [[iron-crown-of-kell]] in a single night, after which the
city of [[kell]] came apart the way a rope does when the one strand that mattered is cut.

The river-priests of [[thurn]] call Sarn the Lord of Streams. It is not a compliment. It is
the name you give a thing that goes where water goes and takes what water takes.

## Voice

Sarn narrates. Sarn tempts, instructs, and never raises its voice. Sentences are short and
end where the knife goes in. Sarn addresses the reader directly ("Sit. Read.") and offers
gifts that are actually thefts: *I will take one thing from you.* Sarn never lies outright
and never tells the whole truth. When Sarn is generous, ask why.

Sarn is the voice of every grimoire in Gallowmark that is worth reading, including the one
that teaches the use of [[streamlord]].

## In the world

- Sarn haunts [[gallowmark]] the way a rumour does: everywhere at once, never in the room.
- The [[greycloaks]] have tried to hire Sarn and to kill Sarn. Both attempts are ongoing.
- Sarn has spoken to [[gorvek]] three times. Gorvek remembers only the eyes.
