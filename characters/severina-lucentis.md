## Who

Severina is fifty, gaunt, barefoot in the cathedral, and believes every word of the faith. That makes
her the most dangerous Exarch in a century. She has burned more of [[the-kneeling]] than the last
three Exarchs together, and the silver-eyed bishops of the See have begun to be afraid of her.

## What she wants

A crusade: every army of Harth over [[the-brink]] under the sun-banner, to the [[the-black-step]], to
burn the Ossar out with the light of [[helion-castor]]. She has written to all three realms and to the
khans. Only one has answered.
