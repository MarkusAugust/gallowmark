## Who

Marcus was sent out of the palace at four, with a purse and no name, and grew up carrying
water in a free company. He took his name from the first weapon he was given. At thirty-five he
is broad, scarred, and the best captain in [[crownhold]], and when he takes his helmet off,
the old men of the court go quiet, because he has [[aurelian-vasthorne]]'s face at forty.

## Voice

A soldier's: short, practical, and funny at the wrong moment. He swears by [[nevermourn]].
