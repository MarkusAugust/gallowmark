## Who

Drusk holds the bridge at [[hookford]], and with it the tithe-books of the middle Mark. He
is the Greycloak who carries a hook, keeps it sharp, and has used it twice; he says the
second time was the last. He is younger than [[tolm]], and where Tolm apologises, Drusk
writes it down. The order finds the two of them useful in the same way a smith finds a
hammer and a file useful, and has never put them in one room.

## The books

Every tithe in the middle Mark goes into Drusk's books, and the books hang from the hooks on
the bridge where the town can see them. He reads from them at the Rope-Moot on
[[hangmoot]], politely, and his is the longest list. The hangmen say his books are a poor
copy of a ledger older than [[kell]], and Drusk has never asked which.

## In the world

- He has never met [[gorvek]]. He has written the price on Gorvek's head into the books
  every winter since the grey, in the same hand.
- He was the first Greycloak to ask, in writing, why Tolm let a man go at the
  [[wallgate]]. He has not had an answer, and has not asked again.
- Since [[the-ebb]] the books have a new column. He has not said what it counts.
