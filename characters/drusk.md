## Who

Drusk is what the [[greycloaks]] look like when they are working. Grey cloak,
clean boots, a hook he has never been seen to use, and manners. He rides into a
village alone, finds the gate somebody forgot, and sits down inside it. By
evening the village pays tithe and believes it decided to. He has done this
more times than any captain living and has never raised his voice.

## Voice

Drusk is polite. He thanks people for their tithe. He asks after their children
by name, and the names are correct, because he keeps the rolls himself. That is
the horror of him, and he knows it, and it is why he does it.

## In the world

- The price on [[gorvek]]'s head is written in Drusk's hand. He raises it every
  winter and has never paid it, and considers both facts a kind of bookkeeping.
- He has sat in the gate of a village in [[bonereach]] exactly once. The
  village closed the gate on him, and he still speaks well of it.
