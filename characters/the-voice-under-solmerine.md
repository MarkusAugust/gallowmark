## What it is

Under [[solmerine]], below the palace cellars where [[seraphine-vasthorne]] broke her father's ring,
there are older cellars, Vahrish, and below those older ones still. Since [[the-fraying]] something
there speaks. Kneelers who go down hear it. They come up with instructions, and the instructions are
the same in every cell in Harth, in the same words, though no messenger has carried them.

## What the hangmen think

That it is a [[the-stonesleepers]], woken in the stone under the city by the breaking of the ring,
and that the cellar where Seraphine knelt was a door. [[ulf-eightrope]] wants to burn Solmerine down
to the bedrock to find out. [[lucius-vasthorne]] has declined, politely.
