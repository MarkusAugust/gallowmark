## Who

Corve got to [[the-iron-hall]] first, years before [[rauk]] dived it, and he sat down in a
chair beside the drowned king and the ledger open on its stand, to rest. He is still resting.
His mail has rusted into one piece with him in it. The dark and the salt have taken most of
what he knew: the name of his village, the faces of his children. They have not taken the
reason he came down. He is sitting beside it, and [[the-standing]] have let him, because he
has never touched it.

Every page of the ledger in the hall has his name on it, because he has not moved. That is
what the ledger counts.

## Voice

Corve says how long. It is the one thing he still knows.

## The hold

The ledger calls whoever holds the most of the Iron Hall the **Sitter in the Iron Hall**.
Nobody has taken the title from Corve, because nobody who reached the chair has wanted to sit
in it after looking at him.
