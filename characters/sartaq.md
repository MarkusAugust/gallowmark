## Who

Sartaq is forty, grey at the temples, and has fought the [[ossar]] on open grass more often than any
living man. He knows what pushed [[the-hoofborn]] west. He wants the khanate to hold [[the-brink]] and
let the three realms of Crownhold bleed each other. He shares the throne with [[bayan]] because their
mother split it, and because the clans would not follow either alone.
