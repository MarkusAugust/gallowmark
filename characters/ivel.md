## Who

Ivel is old, wet to the knees, and has not given a reason for anything in thirty years. Ask why, and you get a name. The names are always right.

## What she does

She keeps the ford's shrine to [[thurn]], sells the river's charms, and sends those who ask the wrong questions north to [[ulf-eightrope]].
