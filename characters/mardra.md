## Who

Mardra went into the grey a midwife and came out of it something the Ashborn will not put a
word to. She is not old. She looks it in some lights. She lives on the rim of [[greyhollow]]
in a house with its back to the smoke, and she goes in when she pleases, which is the thing
nobody else can do.

## Before

The Mark thinks she was a midwife of the east who went into the grey. She was. Before that she
was something else, a long way south and a long time ago: one of [[the-leech-mothers]] of the
[[feverfen]], who went further into [[the-black-step]] for her last lesson than any Leech-Mother
before or since, and came out with a very long life and a great deal she was not meant to know.
She walked north over three hundred years. Her pactmarks are fewer than they should be because
what she paid, she paid inside the temple, and it was not in coin.

## Why everyone needs her

She is the only one in Harth who understands all of it: [[the-first-knot]], the fire under the
Mark, [[the-unborn]], what the [[ossar]] want and why [[the-nine-irons]] matter. Kings send to
her. [[the-ninth-rope]] sends to her. [[the-kneeling]] send to her, and she sends them back with
something missing. She tells everyone the truth, at a price, and lets them decide what to do with
it.

## What she sells

Things from the Hollow and the dark years: a name you lost in it, a winter you do not remember, the last thing a
dead man said. She takes payment in kind, and what she takes is always something you did not
know you had until it was gone. The [[river-priests]] say this makes her a thief. Mardra says it
makes her a merchant, and asks what the difference is, and the priests do not have a name
for it.

## Voice

Mardra explains. She is the only one in the Mark who does, and it is the most frightening thing
about her. Everything she says is true, checkable, and slightly too much.

## In the world

- She says she has seen [[sarn]]'s face and forgotten it on purpose. Nobody can prove either
  half.
- She has offered [[gorvek]] the answer to what he ate in the grey. He walked away. She says
  he will be back, and that this is not a threat.
- [[tolm]] will not tithe her. The [[greycloaks]] have not asked him why.
- Since [[the-ebb]] she has begun selling things she says came up out of [[kell]], and says the
  fire under the Hollow and the warm iron under the hall are one fire.
