## Who

Hrok is grey-bearded, one-handed and still the hardest man in the north. He lost the hand at
the [[wallgate]], to a Greycloak hook, and took the hook home with the arm still in it. Seven
clans stand behind him.

## Voice

Hrok laughs before he answers, and the answer is usually a threat.

## In the world

- He fought beside [[gorvek]] one winter at the Wallgate, against the toll-house, and they
  lost, and drank for three days after. Years later Gorvek came through his hall going north,
  past [[brandfell]], and asked for nothing but a horse. Hrok gave him two.
