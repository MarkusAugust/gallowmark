## The ledger

Every ledger in the [[brinevaults]] counts lines. The first ledger, in the deepest of the
[[strongrooms]], counts names: everyone who ever owed Kell anything, and what was taken in lieu,
and what is still owed. The rope on [[hangmoot]] that went slack forty-one years ago is being
drawn tight. The first ledger is how the rope knows whom to draw.

## Now

The clerk is at the desk, writing. The pen does not stop for you; it writes you down. Those who
have come back up from the strongrooms say the clerk looked up only once, and said their name,
and that they had not given it.
