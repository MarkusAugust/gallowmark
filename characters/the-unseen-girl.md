## The story

Every version is different, which is how you know it is about [[sarn]]. In all of them a girl of
the canals of [[harrowmere]], no older than twelve, put her hand into Sarn's own pocket and took
something out, and Sarn did not notice until the next morning. [[the-honest-company-of-takers]]
keep her a seat at their high table, empty.

## What she took

The stories do not agree. A key. A face. The second ruby, before Sarn could hide it. Sarn, asked,
smiles, and does not change the subject, which is worse.
