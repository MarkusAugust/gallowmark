## Who

He stood in the second rank of the king's guard in [[the-iron-hall]] from the night the crown went
empty until a morning in year 150, when he sat down on the warm floor and asked for the sky. Salt
has eaten his face to the bone. He does not remember his name. He remembers rain.

## What became of him

[[corve]], who sat down long before him, asked that he be walked up through the
[[brinevaults]] to the light. Whether he is still standing in it, nobody on the coast will say.
