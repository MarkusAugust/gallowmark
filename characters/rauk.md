## Who

Rauk is the best diver on the drowned coast, which means the best in the Mark, which means
the only one who has gone down through the iron roof of [[kell]]'s hall and come back up.
She is salt-marked: white lines on her arms where the deep cold got in and stayed. She is
not tall. She has broken a Greycloak's arm on the shingle at [[wrackhead]] and been forgiven
for it, which is rarer than the dive.

## The crown

She has touched the [[iron-crown-of-kell]]. It is still on the king. She says the three
sockets are empty and cold and the iron is warm, and she says nothing else about it, and she
has stopped going down. The coast folk say [[vorr]] told her something. Rauk says the sea
does not talk. It keeps.

## Voice

Rauk speaks like a diver: short, between breaths, saving the air. She does not swear by the
Ashfall, which never reached the coast. She swears by the pile.

## In the world

- She pulled [[brim]] out of the water at Wrackhead eleven years ago and has kept the child
  since, and does not say what else came up with it.
- Since [[the-ebb]] the [[greycloaks]] have offered her the fort's weight in coin to go down
  once more. She has said no in a way they have accepted.
