## Who

Sorgan is twenty-four, lean, scarred across both cheeks by the wind, and has never lost a fight.
When he was twelve a storm came west across [[the-grass-sea]] and he rode into it, against it, for
three days, and came out the other side. The tribes call him Windbreaker, and the old women say
the wind that pushes the [[the-hoofborn]] west every generation has met its rider.

## What he wants

The west. Not to raid it this time. To keep it.
