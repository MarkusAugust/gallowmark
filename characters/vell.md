## Who

Vell was born under the bridge at [[hookford]] and has not worn a shoe since. He is young
for a river-priest, wet to the knee like the rest, and unwelcome at every table in town
except the ones with a death in the family. He walks the two rivers from the ford to the
coast and back each season, and he keeps the names.

## The naming

When the water first went out from Old [[kell]], the coast folk sent to the [[river-priests]]
for a name. Vell had already given it. He had stood on the bridge at Hookford a month before
and said the word *Ebb* to the river, and the river had gone down a finger under his feet.
He says this is not prophecy. He says a name is a grip, and he took hold of something before
it had finished happening.

## Voice

Names, never reasons. Ask Vell why the sea is going out and he will say [[vorr]]. Ask him
what to do and he will say [[thurn]]. Ask him what [[sarn]] wants and he will look at you a
long time and say your own name.

## In the world

- He named [[the-ebb]], and the river-priests are not sure he had the right.
- He has met [[gorvek]] and named the thing Gorvek carries to his face. Gorvek did not
  thank him.
- He named the child at [[wrackhead]] [[brim]], and would not say why.
