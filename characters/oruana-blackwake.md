## Who

Oruana was born in a reed village of the [[feverfen]] and sold at nine to a Harrowmere galley.
At sixteen she led the rowers up through the deck with their chains in their hands. She kept the
ship. Twenty years on she has thirty, and the reavers of [[the-teeth]] call her queen, which she
allows and does not answer to.

## Voice

Soft, vowel-rich, fen-accented, and she never raises it. She has never needed to.

## What she does

She takes Harrowmere's slave galleys, opens the holds, and gives every freed rower a choice: her
deck, or the road to [[saltgrave]]. Most choose the road. Those who choose the deck are
[[blackwakes-own]].
