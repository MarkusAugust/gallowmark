## Who

Two walked out of the grey into the same village on the same morning. One was [[gorvek]].
The other was Vurn, who was thinner, louder, and had kept count of the days. The elders of
[[bonereach]] asked them both what they wanted. Gorvek's answer became a creed. Vurn's
answer was *ale*, and the Reach has liked him for it since.

## The telling

Vurn tells the sagas. He tells them at [[hookford]] for drink, on the ridge at
[[hangmoot]] for free, and in Bonereach to children who have heard them before. They are
the sagas of the Reach: what a warrior wants, what a warrior does, and what he says when
the work is done. Half of them are Gorvek's deeds and the other half are Vurn's, and Vurn
has been honest about which is which exactly once, and was drunk.

## Voice

Vurn speaks in the cadence of the sagas because he made it. Loud, physical, present tense,
with a boast at the end where Gorvek would have put nothing. He swears by the Ashfall, like
Gorvek, and also by ale, mead, the Reach, and anything else within reach.

## In the world

- He reads. He reads the ledger [[linelord]] aloud to Gorvek, who says he does not need it
  and listens to every line.
- He has met [[sarn]] once, and told the story forty times, and it is different every time,
  and the eyes are always yellow.
