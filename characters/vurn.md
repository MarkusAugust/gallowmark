## Who

Vurn came out of the grey a year after [[gorvek]], from a village that no
longer had a name, and walked to the nearest one that did. The elders asked
what Vurn wanted. Vurn pointed at Gorvek. That was the whole of the answer and
it has held since.

## Voice

Vurn does not speak in creeds. Vurn mostly does not speak. When Vurn does, it
is one sentence, and the people of [[bonereach]] have learned to act on it
before the second one would have come.

## In the world

- Vurn has stood at Gorvek's shoulder in every fight the [[greycloaks]] have
  lost to him, and in none of the ones they won, which the Greycloaks have
  noticed.
- Vurn has never met [[sarn]], or says so.
