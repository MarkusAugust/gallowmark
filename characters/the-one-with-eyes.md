## Who

The [[the-blindkin]] are born without eyes in the temple of [[the-underhollow]], and kneel at the
crack because they cannot see what kneels back. In year 150 one of them was born with eyes. They
put her in a niche in the far wall and began to decide.

## Since

She was walked up the stair before they decided, and lives with [[mardra]], who explains things to
her. The first thing she saw was the smoke over [[greyhollow]]. She says it is beautiful.
