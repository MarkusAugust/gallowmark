## Who

Fisk is twenty-two, small, quick, gap-toothed, and was a nobody on the shingle at [[wrackhead]] until
he put a pick through the packing behind a wall in the [[brinevaults]] that everyone had walked past
for a century. What was behind it made him rich. The ledger [[linelord]] named him holder of more
ground than anyone, and [[drykell]] called him Linelord.

## Voice

Cheerful, fast, never quite serious. He knows the title goes to whoever holds the most, and that
somebody is always trying.
