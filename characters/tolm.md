## Who

Tolm is the Greycloak the stories mean when they say a captain rides in alone and sits down.
He is fifty, thin, grey before the cloak was, and polite in the way of a man who has never
once been refused. He does not carry a hook. He says he is no good with it, which is true,
and that he does not need one, which is also true. He keeps the toll-house at [[wallgate]]
and has kept it since the grey.

## Voice

Tolm apologises. He apologises for the tithe, the weather, the price on [[gorvek]]'s head
and the fact that it will be raised again in winter. Every apology is sincere and none of
them changes anything. Ask him a question and he will thank you for it.

## In the world

- He has taken forty villages through the open gate. He remembers every one by the name of
  the person who let him in.
- He has met Gorvek once, across the toll-house table, and let him go. The [[greycloaks]]
  have not asked why. He is waiting for them to.
- He does not go near [[greyhollow]], and is the only Greycloak who will say so.
- Since [[the-ebb]] the order has sent him south for the first time. He apologised to the
  toll-house.
