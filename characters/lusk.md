## Who

Lusk is small, sharp-faced, dressed in grey, and does not wear gold, because, she says,
people who are owed do not need to. She has buried three husbands and two brothers and
written each of them off in the ledger the same afternoon.

## Voice

Lusk talks in sums. Everything has a price, and she tells you yours before you ask.
