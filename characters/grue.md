## Who

Where the [[lowstreets]] narrow to one lane between two fallen houses there is a toll-house
that kept its roof, and in its door sits Grue. He is large, wet to the waist at every tide,
and he learned the trade from watching [[tolm]] at the Wallgate: sit in the door, name a
price, be sorry about nothing. He takes a tenth of whatever goes down past him and a tenth of
whatever comes back up.

He has never been below the Lowstreets. He does not need to go to the ground. The ground
comes to him.

## Voice

Grue names a price. He names it before you ask, and it is always a tenth.

## The hold

The ledger calls whoever holds the most of the Lowstreets the **Lord of the Lowstreets**.
Grue has been called it so long that the pickers think it is his name.
