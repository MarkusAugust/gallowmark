## Who

Gorvek is what [[bonereach]] makes of the ones it does not kill. He was a boy when the ash
came down; he was a man when it cleared, and nobody in the village he walked back into asked
him what he had eaten in between. He is huge, scarred along the left forearm where a
Greycloak's hook took a strip of him, and quiet in the way of people who have decided that
most words are a kind of cowardice.

His hands are scarred by fire. The Reach knows which fire: the ash came down hot the first
winter, and he dug his mother out of it with his hands, too late.

## What he wants

Gold, wine, a warm bed and someone in it, a good horse, a fight he can win, and the
[[greycloaks]] dead. In the dark years the order tithed the grain out of the Reach and let
the villages starve, and the captain who took his village's tithe wore a hook. He has been
taking his tithe back ever since, one Greycloak at a time.

He has been a reaver on the coast road, a thief in [[hookford]], a sword for hire to anyone
who paid before the fight, and once, for a winter, a captain of the hire himself. He left
that with the payroll. Some say he will be a king. He says a crown is a thing you take
because it is there.

## Voice

Gorvek speaks in creeds: short, physical, in the present tense. He answers questions with
what he wants, not with what he thinks. He never explains a joke and rarely makes one on
purpose. When he swears, he swears by the Ashfall, never by a god; he has met the
river-priests of [[thurn]] and was not impressed.

He is the hero of the stories, not their narrator. That is [[sarn]]'s work, and Gorvek knows
it and dislikes it.

## In the world

- He carries a long northern sword he took off a Greycloak captain, and has not named it,
  because naming is river-priest work.
- The [[greycloaks]] have a price on him that they raise every winter and never pay.
- He has met Sarn three times and seen a face none of the times. Sarn owes him for the
  second time, and knows it.
- He walked out of the grey beside [[vurn]], who tells the sagas Gorvek will not, and reads
  him the ledger [[linelord]] that he says he does not need.
- When the sea went out from [[kell]] he was at [[wrackhead]] before the Greycloaks had
  doubled the fort, and he went down first, alone, for the crown. He came up with something
  in a sack and has not shown anyone what.

## The end, which is not one

In the thirty-first winter after the ash cleared he rode north past [[brandfell]] with two
horses and the sack, and was not seen again. That is [[gorveks-last-road]]. Every bastard line
in the north says it is his.
