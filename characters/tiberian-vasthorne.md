## Who

Tiberian is thirty, golden-haired, tall, and good at everything that is done in front of a
crowd: riding, speaking, weeping at funerals. He has never read a document he signed. His
councillors read them for him, and his councillors are paid by the House of Salt.

## Voice

Warm, sincere, and a little too loud. He means it. That is the worst of it.
