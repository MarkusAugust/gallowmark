## Who

Torr-Halaq is old, upright, veiled to the eyes in black, and leads four thousand tents across the
[[glassands]]. His name was on the old list of the emperors' household, carved on a gate of
[[old-vahr]]; his ancestor was a slave there. He took it back.

## What he knows

Why the [[the-hadd]] never go near Old Vahr. The Hadd were the emperors' slaves on the last night.
They were standing on the glass of [[ahl-sereth]] when the asking was made, and they saw what was
given. They walked away, at night, and have walked only at night since.
