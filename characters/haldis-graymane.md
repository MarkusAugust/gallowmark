## Who

Haldis was hanged at sixteen as her oath and cut down at the last breath, like every hangman, and
the scar at her throat is the darkest on the ridge. She has been Reeve for twenty years. She is the
first woman to hold the office and the first Reeve since the Broken Years to be frightened.

## What she knows

Everything [[the-ninth-rope]] knows about [[the-first-knot]]. That the ninth rope has hung slack
since [[the-drowning-of-kell]]. That the [[the-burning-of-the-rolls]] loosens it with every fire.
That [[the-kneeling]] are winning. And lately, that the [[greycloaks]], who never shut a gate, may
not be on her side.
