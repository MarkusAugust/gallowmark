## Who the captains meet

Few captains of the [[greycloaks]] ever meet her, and those who do are changed by it. She is tall,
grey-eyed, pale as milk, and beautiful in a way that makes men and women forget what they came to
say. She is warm, attentive, amused. She touches your hand when she speaks. She reads the books,
all of them, and remembers every line, and she has never written one.

## What she is

She is an [[ossar]]. When [[the-first-knot]] was tied and her people went under the earth, she did
not. She put on a human face and stayed, and she has worn it for three thousand years, under
other names: a commander of [[the-tenth-legion]] in [[old-vahr]], a widow of the Broken Years, a
lady of the order. Her skin is cold. She does not sleep. She does not age.

## What she wants

The order holds gates. It has held them since Vahr, for a tenth, politely, everywhere: never
ruling, always there, never shutting anything. That is her work. A knot holds what is below. A
gate held open lets it out. She is waiting for the rope to give, and when it does, every gate in
Harth will already be open.

## Voice

Low, warm, unhurried, and very close to your ear. She asks about you. She is always interested.

## In the world

- [[mardra]] knows what she is. They have met twice, and neither has said what passed.
- [[sarn]] took a tenth of the crown in one night. Ishara Grey takes a tenth of everything,
  forever. The two of them know each other very well.
- [[the-ninth-rope]] have never suspected her. The Reeve [[haldis-graymane]] has begun to.
