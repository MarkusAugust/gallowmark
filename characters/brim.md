## Who

Eleven years ago, in the grey, on a still day at low water, [[rauk]] came up from Old
[[kell]] with a child under one arm that had not been there when she went down. It was
perhaps three. It is perhaps nine now, and it should be fourteen. It has never spoken. It
has salt-white eyes that see in the dark and a habit of looking at the sea as if waiting for
someone to finish a sentence.

## What the coast decided

The coast folk decided, without a meeting, that the child was the last of the kings of Kell,
given back. They call it Brim, which is the name [[vell]] gave it, and they leave fish at
Rauk's door. Nobody has proved a word of it. Nobody has needed to. It is believed, and in the
Mark that is a kind of iron.

## In the world

- The [[greycloaks]] have offered to foster the child at the fort on [[wrackhead]]. Rauk
  declined. The offer stands, and is polite.
- Since [[the-ebb]] the child has walked to the waterline every morning and stood where the
  quay-stones are showing, and the water has been a little further out each time.
- [[sarn]], asked about the child, did not change the subject. That is the only time.
