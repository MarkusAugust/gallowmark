## Who

Brodd came down from [[emberstead]] with a hammer, an anvil on a cart and a debt he does not talk about. Broad, short, burnt to the elbows.

## What he does

He makes and mends iron for anyone with marks, and takes the Greycloaks' work first because [[aske]] asks politely.
