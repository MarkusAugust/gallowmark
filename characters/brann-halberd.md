## Who

Brann is thirty-eight, scarred from brow to jaw on the left side by something that came over
[[the-brink]] at night, and the best captain the March has had since [[marcus-halberd]]. The company
chose him by acclaim. He has not smiled since the Brink, and the March has stopped expecting it.

## Voice

Few words, all orders. He swears by [[nevermourn]].
