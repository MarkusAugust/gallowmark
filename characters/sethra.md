## Who

Sethra does not stop anyone in the [[brinevaults]]. She sits among the clerks' shelves with
a lamp turned low and lets you pass. Then she waits. Most of the ones who go down past her do
not come back, and in a season or a year she goes down a little way after them and brings up
what they carried. Everything she owns was carried into Kell by someone braver.

She has never fought for the vaults. Nobody who fought her for them is still alive to say
whether she would have won.

## Voice

Sethra speaks quietly and about afterwards. She is always certain there will be one.

## The hold

The ledger calls whoever holds the most of the Brinevaults the **Keeper of the
Brinevaults**. It has called her that through every one of the people she waited out.
