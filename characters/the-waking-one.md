## The stone

The [[the-stonesleepers]] went into the stone when the knot was tied. Most of them lie under the
[[glassands]] and the [[feverfen]], far down. One did not go far. It lies in the black stone at the
heart of the [[the-underhollow]], at the lip of the crack, where its servants built their ring of
columns so they could kneel by it.

## Now

The [[the-blindkin]] of the temple are sweeping faster. The stone at the lip of the crack is
warm, and it has a shape in it that it did not have when [[mardra]] first went down. She says it
has opened its hand.
