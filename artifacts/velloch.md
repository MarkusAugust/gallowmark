## The stone

Velloch held the second river, the one that meets the first at [[hookford]]. It is the ruby
[[sarn]] has never quite said is gone. The divers and the pickers believe it is still in
[[the-iron-hall]], somewhere the king could reach. [[corve]] is sitting beside something.

## What it does

Set in one of [[the-nine-irons]], it holds the ring shut, and the ring holds a rope of
[[the-first-knot]]. Out of the iron, the stories disagree. Some say it gives the holder grip on
the river it held. Some say it gives the river grip on the holder.
