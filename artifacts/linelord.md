## What it is

In the world, LineLord is a ledger. Not of coin; of lines. Every wall in [[gallowmark]] is
laid one line of stone at a time, and the ledger remembers who laid each line and how long
it has stood, and nothing else. It does not say whether the wall was any good. It says who
held the ground, and for how long, and that is the whole of what it says. The last king of
[[kell]] kept it in the iron-roofed hall and read it the winter before the night, looking
for a wall that would hold. The [[greycloaks]]' tithe-books are a poor copy of it. Brother
[[nask]] named it, and then stopped talking about it, as the [[river-priests]] do.

[[vurn]] reads it aloud. [[gorvek]] says he does not need it, and listens to every line.

## The rule

The ledger counts lines and days. It is not a measure of worth, and the Reach has never
mistaken it for one: a wall nobody dares touch stands as long in the ledger as a wall that
earned its place.

Outside the world, LineLord is a command-line tool that reads `git blame` and reports who
holds which line of a codebase and for how long. This entry is where the world and the tool
meet. Its farewells are the sagas of the Reach in Vurn's cadence; its names are
Gallowmark's; its rule is the one above, and it stands on every screen.
