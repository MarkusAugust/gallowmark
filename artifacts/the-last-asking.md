## The tablet

A slab of black stone the height of a man, written on both sides in the emperors' hand, carried
out onto [[ahl-sereth]] on the last night and read aloud. When the fire came, the stone went white
and half of it ran into the glass. What is left stands in the throne-room of [[old-vahr]], before
[[qeth-ummar]], who reads it every night.

## The contract

[[the-gilded]] bought the debts of Vahr. They hold that the Last Asking is one of them: a contract,
with a price not yet paid in full. They have sent three expeditions to copy it. One came back.
