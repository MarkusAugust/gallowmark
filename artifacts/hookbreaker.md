## The sword

A long, plain northern blade, black iron, a hand-and-a-half grip wrapped in horse-leather.
[[gorvek]] took it off a [[greycloaks]] captain on the coast road and never gave it a name,
because naming is river-priest work. The songs did it for him. It broke more Greycloak hooks than
anyone could count, and the songs count them anyway.

## After

It went north with him on [[gorveks-last-road]]. Every hall in the north and half the holdfasts of
the Mark have a Hookbreaker over the fire. None of them is it.
