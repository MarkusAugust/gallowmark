## The rings

Nine ropes hang on [[hangmoot]] and nine iron rings were forged to hold them shut, one for each.
The first men gave them to their chiefs to wear, so that the oath would sit on the heads of those
who ruled. The emperors of [[old-vahr]] wore them as crowns, under the gold. When the empire broke,
the rings went with the provinces.

## Where they are

- The ninth was the [[iron-crown-of-kell]], set with [[tharsk]], [[velloch]] and [[imryn]]. It is
  empty.
- One is on the head of [[aurelian-vasthorne]], under the gold, and [[seraphine-vasthorne]] wants
  it.
- Some are in [[harrowmere]]: [[aleid-van-der-haalst]] buys every iron ring that comes to market,
  real or not.
- The rest are lost, or worn by people who do not know what they are.
