## The stone

Imryn held the third river, the one that runs shallow at [[fordham]]. Of the three, it is the one
everybody agrees about: [[sarn]] still has it. Where Sarn keeps it, nobody knows. The
[[the-honest-company-of-takers]] say it is the only thing their first master never sold.

## What it does

Set in one of [[the-nine-irons]], it holds the ring shut, and the ring holds a rope of
[[the-first-knot]]. Out of the iron, the stories disagree. Some say it gives the holder grip on
the river it held. Some say it gives the river grip on the holder.
