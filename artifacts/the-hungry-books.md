## The books

The emperors of [[old-vahr]] wrote down how to ask, so that their heirs would not have to learn
it from the [[ossar]]. The books survived the fire. Nobody knows how many there are. Each one is
bound in pale leather, written in a hand no scribe of Harth can match, and opens most easily at
the page its reader most wants.

## The hunger

A Hungry Book does not stay where it is put. Locked in a chest, it is found in a market. Burned,
it is found in the ashes, a little scorched. It always ends in the hands of someone who will
read it, and the first asking in it is always small.

## In the world

- [[the-ninth-rope]] keeps eleven under the gallows at [[hangmoot]], in iron, and counts them
  every morning. Some mornings there are ten.
- [[the-kneeling]] copy them, which is a crime the books seem to enjoy.
