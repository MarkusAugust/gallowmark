## What it is

In the world, Streamlord is a thing that makes a stream obey. Not by damming it. By telling
it, and being believed. [[gorvek]] carries it and does not know what it is, only what it does;
[[sarn]] narrates the grimoire that teaches its use and takes one belief from every reader in
payment.

Outside the world, Streamlord is a software development kit, and this entry is how the
world and the software meet. The grimoire's voice is Sarn's; the creed on its first page is
Gorvek's; the names in it are Gallowmark's.

## The rule

Streamlord carries exactly what the wielder means, and nothing else. The [[river-priests]]
say this is why [[thurn]] allows it: a stream held still is still a stream.
