## What it is

Streamlord is not a thing in [[gallowmark]]. Nobody in the Mark carries it, wields it, or
has heard of it. It is a software development kit, and its documentation is written as a
grimoire, and that grimoire borrows the Mark's voices for its epigraphs: [[sarn]] narrates
it, the creed on its first page is [[gorvek]]'s, and the names in it are Gallowmark's.

## The rule

What the grimoire says about streams is about the software. Nothing in the canon depends on
it, and no character in the Mark can make a river obey.
