## The stone

Tharsk held the first river in its bed, the cold one that comes down out of the north through the
[[wallgate]]. The rumour of [[harrowmere]] is that it was sold there the year after Kell drowned, to
the House of Salt, and that it went by barge to [[the-tenth-palace]] and was never seen again.

## What it does

Set in one of [[the-nine-irons]], it holds the ring shut, and the ring holds a rope of
[[the-first-knot]]. Out of the iron, the stories disagree. Some say it gives the holder grip on
the river it held. Some say it gives the river grip on the holder.
