## The crown

Iron, not gold. The kings of [[kell]] wore iron because gold was for people with something
to prove. Three rubies were set in it, one for each of the grey rivers that met at the city,
and the river-priests of [[thurn]] said that while the stones sat in the iron, the rivers sat
in their beds.

## The theft

[[sarn]] took all three in a single night. Nobody saw the face. The rivers left their beds.
Whether the crown held the water or only the belief in the crown did, Kell drowned either
way, and the empty iron is somewhere under it.
