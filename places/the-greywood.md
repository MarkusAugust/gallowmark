## The wood

West of [[hookford]] the old forest of the Mark stood in the path of the ash. It died standing.
Thirty years on, the trunks are still there, grey and bare, hard as bone, and the wind goes
through them with a sound like breathing. Nothing grows under them. Nothing nests in them.

## Who lives there

Outlaws, because the [[greycloaks]] do not go in. Charcoal-burners, because grey wood burns hot.
And in the deep of it, past where the outlaws go, things the outlaws do not name.
