## The city

A gorge in the hills above [[harrowmere]], walled at both ends, its houses cut into the rock
faces in tiers. Under the gate is the grave: a pit full of salt, a handful from every one of
[[the-saltless]] who ever came in. They walk over it every day.

## Its enemies

[[the-gilded]] have sent three armies up the gorge. The bones of the third are still in it. The
[[greycloaks]] have never been hired against Saltgrave, and the order has never said why.
