## The stair

The last king of Kell did not trust the doors of his own hall. He had a stair
cut through the wall behind the throne, up toward the iron roof, and had it
bricked over so it looked like wall. He never used it. The night came on him in
his chair. The packing has filled the brickwork, and the stair is still there,
going up.
