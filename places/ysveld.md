## The land

Nobody in Harth has been there and come back to say so. The ships come from the west, a few each
summer, white-sailed and too fast, into [[harrowmere]], and their people call it Ysveld.

## The people

Tall, fair, soft-voiced, with names like Ilvar, Saelith and Corvanne. They are unfailingly
courteous. They buy iron, salt, Ossar bones and, once, a [[the-hungry-books]], and pay in silver
that does not tarnish. They do not drink, do not marry, and do not answer questions about home.
