## The village

Forty houses built low against the wind off the hollow, walls of stone, roofs of turf laid over
ash. The smoke of [[greyhollow]] stands a long stone's throw from the last house. The Ashen keep
goats, grow a grey barley nobody else can grow, and trade with [[mardra]], whose house is a mile
round the rim.

## Custom

Visitors are given water at the gate, never bread, until the elders have looked at them. The
dead are laid in the smoke's edge and not brought back. Every winter outlasted is a new line of
ash in the skin.
