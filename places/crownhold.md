## The kingdom

Crownhold holds the best land in Harth: wheat, vine, the old imperial road and the
river cities along it. Its capital, the city of the same name, is built on the ruins of a
Vahrish provincial seat, and its kings sit on a throne they say was carried out of
[[old-vahr]] the night it burned. It was not. Everyone at court knows it was not.

## The court

The court of Crownhold runs on three things: offices for sale, poison in the wine, and
debts to [[harrowmere]]. The Gilt King [[aurelian-vasthorne]] has outlived four heirs and two queens. The
lords of the provinces raise armies against each other in his name. The [[greycloaks]] farm
the kingdom's taxes for a tenth of them, and the king is grateful, and has never asked what
else they take.

## The cult

In the cellars of the capital the [[the-kneeling]] meet in rooms the city watch is paid not
to find. Some of the people who meet there wear court silk.
