## The city

The fire that ended the empire was whitest here. The towers of Old Vahr stand bent like
candles in a hot room, their stone run into glass. The palace still stands, because it was
built on [[ossar]] foundations, and those did not melt.

## The throne

On the glass throne sits [[ommet]], who says he is the last emperor and was there on the
night. Nobody can prove he was not. The caravans pay him in water to cross his glass.
