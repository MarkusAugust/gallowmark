## The wall

Far north, past the last valley anyone has farmed, the ice stands up in a wall: blue-white,
miles long, higher than the towers of [[crownhold]]. In it, deep, there are shapes. Tall ones,
too tall, standing upright with their arms at their sides, the way the [[ossar]] were drawn on
the walls of their temples. Behind them, deeper, larger ones that are not shaped like anything.

## The sleep

The [[rimeclans]] say the shapes are sleeping, not dead, and that the cold is what keeps them
asleep. Since [[brandfell]] split, the north has been warmer. Water runs down the face of the Wall
in summer now. [[clan-skoll]] went there and came back changed.
