## The streets

Below [[quaysteps]] the city is still a city, and every door in it is barred from the
inside. The coast says Kell went home that night and waited for the crown to hold. The
scratches on the doors are on the outside, low, and they are not from water.

When the crown went empty, Kell came apart before the water came in. The streets that
had nothing went up the hill to the streets that had something, with knives. Every house
that had a bar used it.

## The houses

The people inside held the doors against their neighbours, and the sea came in through the
windows instead, and they drowned with their shoulders to the wood. Some of them did not stay
drowned. Nobody has asked them what they ate.
