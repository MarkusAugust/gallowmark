## The streets

Below [[quaysteps]] the city is still a city. The streets run where they ran, half full of
silt and wreck, and the houses stand along them with their roofs gone and their doors shut.
Shut from the inside. The coast folk say Kell did not run that night. It went home, barred
the door, and waited for the crown to hold.

## What lives here

What the sea did not keep, it gave to the things that eat. What it did keep stands in the
doorways: [[the-kept]], who wake when the flood comes and walk with it. The pickers of
[[wrackhead]] work the Lowstreets in pairs and leave a rope back to the steps, and the
rope is the thing they trust.
