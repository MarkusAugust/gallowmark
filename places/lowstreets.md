## The streets

Below [[quaysteps]] the city is still a city, and every door in it is barred from the
inside. The coast says Kell went home that night and waited for the crown to hold. The
scratches on the doors are on the outside, low, and they are not from water.

## The houses

Some of the people inside lived. The sea came, and then the grey came after it, up through
the water, and the people in the barred houses stayed shut in with it for a generation. When
it lifted they were still there. Nobody has asked them what they ate.
