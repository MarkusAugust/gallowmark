## The land

Ice in the north, glass in the south, rot in the east, and in between a thousand years of
kingdoms that are each sure they are what is left of [[old-vahr]]. Nobody calls it one
place except the people who are not from it. Harth is what the empire looked like
after [[the-fall-of-vahr]]: a plate dropped on a stone floor.

## From north to south

- [[rimewold]], beyond the Wallmountains: ice, clans and the smoke of [[brandfell]].
- [[gallowmark]], the borderland under the mountains, where the world is tied shut at
  [[hangmoot]].
- [[crownhold]], the gilt kingdom that claims Vahr's throne and is eaten from the inside.
- [[harrowmere]], the canal-city of merchant princes, the richest and rottenest place in the
  Sunder.
- [[feverfen]], the swamp-jungle of the south-east coast, over the Ossar's drowned temples.
- [[the-teeth]], the reaver islands off the fen coast.
- [[glassands]], the desert of fused sand where Vahr's heartland burned.

## What holds it together

Nothing. The roads are Vahr's and nobody mends them. The coin is Harrowmere's and nobody
trusts it. The [[greycloaks]] are in every kingdom and rule none of them. Under all of it,
the [[the-unborn]] are bound, and the binding is a rope in the north that one thief has
already loosened.
