## The land

East of the eastern hills the trees stop and the grass begins, and it does not stop. A sea of it,
to the height of a horse's chest, moving under the wind for a thousand miles. The [[the-hoofborn]]
live on it, and nothing else does that the Hoofborn have not decided to leave alive.
