## The town

Two of the three grey rivers meet at Hookford. The ford was there before the town, and the
bridge before the [[greycloaks]]. It is the only place in the middle Mark with a market, a
smith who takes coin, and a hall where a stranger can sleep with a door between him and the
wind. The people of the Mark distrust it accordingly.

## The hooks

The Greycloaks hung their hooks on the bridge the year they came, one per span, and hang the
tithe-books from them. Nobody has ever been hanged from the bridge. Everybody knows that they
could be, and pays.

## The road

Every road in the Mark passes through Hookford: down from [[wallgate]] and [[bonereach]], up
from [[wrackhead]] and the coast, east to the rim of [[greyhollow]], and the ridge road to
[[hangmoot]]. If [[the-ebb]] pulls the Mark to the coast, it will pull it through here.
