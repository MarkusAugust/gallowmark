## The hall

The iron roof is the only part of [[kell]] the coast can see, and since [[the-ebb]] it stands
clear of the salt a little longer every low water. Under it the king sits with the
[[iron-crown-of-kell]] on his head and three empty sockets in it. The iron is warm.

## The guard

The king's household guard drowned at their posts in full iron, and [[the-standing]] are
what is left of them: two ranks down the length of the hall, salt-white, facing the door.
They do not move while the king sits. Reach for what is his and they do.

## The floor

The flagstones are warm under the silt, warmer the nearer the throne. In the middle of the
floor is the grate of the great cistern, and the water that goes down it comes back up as
steam on a cold day. [[the-heave]] is under the hall, and closest here.

## The ledger

[[linelord]] lies open on its stand at the king's right hand. The last line in it is not the
king's. [[corve]] sits beside it and has not moved in years. Behind the throne, the
[[kings-way]] goes up.
