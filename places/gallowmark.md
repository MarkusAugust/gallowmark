## The land

Gallowmark is named for what its first rulers built before they built anything else. The
gallows are still there on the ridges, most of them empty, none of them taken down. The land
runs from the Wallmountains in the north to the drowned coast in the south, cut through by
grey rivers that the priests of [[thurn]] say are one river pretending to be many.

Nothing here is fertile by accident. The people are hard because the soil is, and they trust
what they can hold. A stranger who can make a thing obey will be respected; a stranger who
explains why will be watched.

## Regions

- [[bonereach]] in the north, where [[gorvek]] comes from.
- [[kell]] on the coast, the drowned city that still counts as a place because the survivors
  refuse to stop saying its name.

## Since the Ashfall

The [[the-ashfall]] came down over the north and stayed a generation. The Mark has been
darker since, and its stories shorter.
