## The coming

In the tenth winter, while the three crowns fought each other and the south was burning,
[[sorgan-windbreaker]] came west over the hills with every tribe of [[the-hoofborn]]. [[the-iron-hundred]]
rode out to meet him and died to the last knight. He did not go home. He took [[marcheast]] and
let it go back to grass.

## In the year 150

The khans of the Saddle Throne hold court in tents in the ruins of the eastern towns, and sit on
their horses to give judgment. The khanate trades horses to all three realms of Crownhold, raids
all three, and is the only power in Harth that has fought the Ossar on open ground and won. They
know now what pushed them west.
