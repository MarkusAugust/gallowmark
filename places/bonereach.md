## The reach

Bonereach is the part of [[gallowmark]] nobody wanted and somebody kept. Scree slopes, birch
so pale it looks skinned, and villages built into the lee of rocks because the wind comes
off the Wallmountains with an opinion. The people of the Reach are the people of the Mark
boiled down: fewer words, harder hands, a longer memory.

## Since the Ashfall

The [[the-ashfall]] fell here first and lifted here last. The Reach lost half its villages
and does not say which half. Those who walked out of the grey, [[gorvek]] among them, are
called the Ashborn behind their backs and never to their faces.

## Custom

The elders of the Reach do not ask a returning warrior where he has been. They ask what he
wants. The answer is the man.
