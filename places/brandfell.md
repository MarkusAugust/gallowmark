## The mountain

Brandfell stands in the Wallmountains a day north of the [[wallgate]], and before the
Ashfall it was only the tallest of them. Then it split from the crown to the knees, and the
fire inside came out, and the sky over [[bonereach]] went black and stayed that way for a
generation. That was [[the-ashfall]].

## Now

It still smokes. The snow does not lie on its south face. The Reach sends nobody up it and
asks nobody who has come down from it where they have been.
