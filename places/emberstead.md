## The town

Since [[brandfell]] split, its south face has been warm. Vents in the rock breathe hot air,
and the snow melts before it lands. The smiths of the north came up to it and built Emberstead
in the vents: stone forges set over cracks in the mountain, fed by the fire under it, no
charcoal needed.

## The work

The best iron in Harth comes out of Emberstead, black, heavy, and slightly warm for a day after
it is quenched. The smiths worship [[the-old-smith-under-the-snow]] and burn something for
him every winter. Some winters it is a smith.
