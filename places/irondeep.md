## The town

Black houses on a black hillside, slag-heaps, and the mouths of the pits. Irondeep has dug iron
for the Mark since before [[kell]] had a quay. The miners are Ashborn, debtors and convicts, and
the [[greycloaks]] take a tenth of the ore at the pithead.

## Since Brandfell

The deep shafts have grown warm. The lowest galleries are too hot to work in summer. The miners
say they hear knocking below the deepest level, regular, like a hammer. The smiths of
[[emberstead]] say they know exactly what that is.
