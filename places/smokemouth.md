## The island

A black cone at the end of the jaw, the furthest of [[the-teeth]] from the coast. It was dead
rock for as long as the reavers remember. The year [[brandfell]] split, it began to smoke. Last
year the top fell in, and now it breathes: a long plume by day, a red glow at night, and the
sea around it warm enough to cook fish.

## The building

On the crater's lip, something is going up: black stone, laid in the old way, too large. Ships
that put in to see do not come back. [[oruana-blackwake]] has forbidden her captains to try.
