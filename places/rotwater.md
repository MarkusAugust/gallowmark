## The port

Stilt-houses over the mud at a river mouth, a quay of rotting timber, a hundred taverns and no
law. The reavers of [[the-teeth]] bring their prizes here, and the buyers of [[harrowmere]] come
masked to buy them back. Lanterns hung on the quay mean trading is safe tonight. When the
lanterns come down, it is not.

## Who is here

Everyone. Fen-folk selling fish and poison, reavers, Harrowmere factors, deserters from
[[the-unpaid-war]], [[the-honest-company-of-takers]] on a working holiday, and the
[[greycloaks]], who keep a small, polite house by the quay and tithe the lantern-oil.
