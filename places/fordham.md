## The town

[[hookford]] has the bridge and the hooks. Fordham has the horses. On the third of the grey
rivers, where it runs shallow over gravel, the fairs of the west Mark have been held since the
ash cleared: horses, cattle, iron from [[irondeep]], grey wood from [[the-greywood]], and swords
for hire.

## The fair

At the spring fair the [[the-holdfast-lords]] hire their seasons of Ashborn, and the Ashborn
look them over the way they look over the horses. More feuds start at Fordham than anywhere in
the Mark, and more end there.
