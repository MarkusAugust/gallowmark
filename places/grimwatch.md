## The keep

Grimwatch is Vahrish, older than anything else in the Mark, a square of black stone where the
imperial road comes down out of the hills into Gallowmark. [[crownhold]] built it as a border
keep. The [[greycloaks]] were hired to garrison it two hundred years ago and never left.

## Now

Whichever of [[the-three-crowns]] holds Grimwatch holds the road north. All three know it.
The captain of the keep has received three letters this winter and answered all three, politely.
