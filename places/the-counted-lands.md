## What is said

South of the [[glassands]] the maps stop. The [[the-hadd]] say the land goes on, green, rich and
ordered, and that the [[ossar]] never went under there, because the knot was tied in the north and
did not reach so far. Men live there, the Hadd say, in neat villages, fed, counted, and taken.

## What is known

Nothing. Once in a generation someone walks out of the south across the glass: thin, quiet, with a
number tattooed on the inside of the wrist. They do not talk about it. They do not stay long.

## How they live

Happily. Fed, well, at peace, and numbered: [[the-count]] decides everything, including when they
are taken.
