## The city

Solmerine stands on the hills above a shallow lake so bright under the summer sun that the court
calls it the sun-sea. The palace of [[aurelian-vasthorne]] is on the highest hill; the cathedral
of [[the-sunward-faith]] on the next; the great houses below; the poor everywhere else.

## Its life

Banquets, processions on [[helions-crown]], duels on the marble stairs, offices sold in the
cathedral's side-rooms, and the cellars where [[the-kneeling]] meet. It is the most beautiful city
in Harth and smells, in summer, of the lake.
