## The place

Bleakshore is a huddle of fishers' huts on the bare coast east of Wrackhead, the poorest on the shore, which pays Vorr first and the Greycloaks second. Its people fish, pay the salt-pile at [[wrackhead]] for [[vorr]], pay the
[[greycloaks]] their tenth, and trust neither.
