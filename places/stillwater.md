## The prison

A square of timber and iron on a hundred piles, a mile out on the black lake. The debtors live
in cages and work at whatever their creditors send out: rope, nets, copying ledgers. Every
month the books are balanced. A debtor whose account will never close is taken to the edge
with a stone and given to the lake.

## The water

It is still around Stillwater. Nobody swims from it, and not because of the distance. The
canal-lurkers of the lake have learned where the food comes from.
