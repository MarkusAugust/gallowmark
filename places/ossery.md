## The city

Ossery was a river city of the old empire, rich, learned and named, the scholars said, after the
[[ossar]] who first built there. It was the first city [[the-kneeling]] took whole. It is ash now.
Nothing grows on it four hundred years on, and the Crownhold road goes round.
