## The place

Ebbmouth is the fishing village at the mouth of the third river, whose harbour has gone dry since the Ebb, and whose boats now lie on mud a mile from the water. Its people fish, pay the salt-pile at [[wrackhead]] for [[vorr]], pay the
[[greycloaks]] their tenth, and trust neither.
