## The pits

The burners of [[the-greywood]] dug their pits deep and roofed them with turf, and when the
wood went grey after [[the-ashfall]] they dug deeper, chasing wood that would burn. The deepest
pits run into each other under the hill. [[kolm]]'s father dug the first of them. Kolm will not
go in.

## What is in them

The ash in the pits never quite went cold. The [[the-kneeling]] found that out, and that
[[knotrats]] breed fast in warm ash and grow teeth for rope. Outlaws who hide in the pits come
out coughing, if they come out. Those who breathe too much of the old smoke stay, and walk.
