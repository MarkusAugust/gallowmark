## The head

A black headland shaped like a fist, with Old [[kell]] under the water at its knuckles. The
[[greycloaks]] built their first fort here, forty years ago, to guard the drowned quays. It
is the smallest fort they hold and the only one they have never left.

## The salt-pile

Below the fort, on the shingle, the coast folk keep a pile of wreck-wood and salt for
[[vorr]]. A plank from every wreck, a handful from every net. The Greycloaks tithe
everything on the coast and have never touched the pile.

## The wreckers

The people of Wrackhead dive. On a still day at low water you can see the iron roof of
Kell's hall, and once a year somebody goes down to it. [[rauk]] has gone down more than
anyone, come up with less each time, and stopped talking about it.

## Since the Ebb

The water is going out. The quays are showing. Since [[the-ebb]] the fort has doubled its
garrison and the salt-pile has doubled in height, and neither side has said why.
