## The realm

When [[the-three-crowns]] were crowned, the capital crowned [[tiberian-vasthorne]], and he kept it:
[[solmerine]], the Saltcoast, Vinalis and the river cities. His line holds them still, a dynasty of
golden kings with beautiful manners and Harrowmere councillors.

## In the year 150

The richest of the three realms and the weakest. Its armies are bought. Its debts are held by
[[aleid-van-der-haalst]]'s descendants. It wants the other two crowns back, and Harrowmere lends it
the money to try, every generation, at interest.
