## The town

When the coast was lifted far enough, Kell came out of the sea for good: quays, streets, vaults
and the iron roof, dry and white with salt. The people who had been going down at low water stopped
leaving. Drykell is what they built: shanties on the [[quaysteps]], taverns in the
[[lowstreets]], gambling dens in the [[brinevaults]].

## The law

There is none, except the ledger. Whoever holds the most ground in [[linelord]] calls themselves
the Linelord, and takes a tithe of every pick. The title changes hands every few years, usually with
a knife. The [[greycloaks]] hold the road out, and take theirs.

## Still digging

Kell had a great many rooms, and the packing still fills most of them. Every week somebody breaks
into one nobody has opened, and every month somebody does not come back from one. Some of the
pickers pay a small tithe at the Iron Hall, to the king who is still sitting in his chair, and
nobody says to whom it goes.
