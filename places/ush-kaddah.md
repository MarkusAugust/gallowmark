## The city

A circle of white walls around a hole in the glass. The well at the heart of Ush-Kaddah is
wide as a market square and goes down past the reach of any rope. The water is cold and never
fails. Around it, the only city in the [[glassands]]: markets, caravanserais, Hadd tents, the
counting-houses of [[harrowmere]], and a small grey chapterhouse of the [[greycloaks]].

## The peace

The name means *the water that does not count*: here no tribe, king or caravan owes anything
for drinking. Blood spilled inside the walls is paid for in water, weighed against the dead, and
the killer goes thirsty for the debt. Even [[the-well-kings]] keep the peace here.
