## The farm

A long-house, barns, fields of grey barley, and the warmest soil in the Mark, because it is the
nearest to the fire. To a traveller it is a good farm, quiet, prosperous, hospitable. There are no
dogs. There are no young children. At night the family goes out into the furrows and kneels with
their foreheads on the warm earth.

## What they do

They listen. They send their own into the smoke to become [[smokewalkers]], and the hungry ones
they keep. They buy [[knotrats]] by the cage. And they are digging, slowly, under the barn, toward
[[the-underhollow]].
