## The temple

In the middle of the [[feverfen]], where the water is deepest and the trees tallest, a black
pyramid stands up out of the swamp in steps, each one the height of a man. Vines cover the lower
steps. The upper ones are clean. Nothing grows on them, and nothing has been seen to clean them.

## The lights

On some nights there are lights at the top: pale, steady, not fire. The fen-folk leave fish,
reed-cloth and sometimes worse on the lowest step at new moon, and in the morning it is gone.
[[the-leech-mothers]] say the [[ossar]] are inside, and that the warmth coming out of the
temple has been rising every year since [[brandfell]] split.
