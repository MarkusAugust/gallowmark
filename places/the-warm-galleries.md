## The galleries

[[irondeep]] has dug iron for the Mark since before [[kell]] had a quay. The ore for the king's
iron in [[the-iron-hall]] came up the old ore-road from these shafts. Since [[brandfell]] the
lowest galleries have grown warm, then hot. The ore down there is soft as wax and glows.

## What is in them

The miners who went down for the warm ore came back up slow and quiet and empty behind the
eyes, and went down again, and stopped coming up. The [[greycloaks]] closed the shafts and
take their tenth from the upper galleries only. In the deepest gallery the iron vein runs
round something that is not iron.
