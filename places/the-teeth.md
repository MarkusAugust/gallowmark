## The islands

Forty black islands in a curve like a broken jaw, steep, hot, with good harbours in their
lee and nothing growing on their windward side. The reaver fleets winter here and raid from
here: the Harrowmere slave galleys, the Crownhold grain ships, the coast towns of the fen.

## The law

There is none but the Captain's Oath: a captain's word, once given, is kept, because a
captain who breaks it is hanged from the mast on Hask's pattern by their own crew. The reavers have
never heard of [[the-first-knot]]. They keep it anyway.
