## The house

Between the palaces of Salt and Tallow stands one more, as fine as either, built of the same
pale stone, and it has no doors. No windows. No water-gate. The canal runs along its walls and
nothing goes in. The people of [[harrowmere]] call whatever lives there the Tenth Prince.

## The payment

Once a year, on the night of [[the-hiring-of-the-wise]], each of [[the-gilded]] sends a sealed
chest by barge, and the barge is tied against the blank wall, and in the morning the chest is
gone. Each house keeps the contents in its own ledger. No house has ever shown another.
