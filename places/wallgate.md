## The pass

The Wallmountains have one gap, and the Mark calls it the Wallgate because nobody in the
Mark wastes a second word. It is a throat of black rock a day's walk long, with the first of
the grey rivers coming down through it cold enough to stop a heart. North of it is whatever
the Ashfall came from. South of it is [[bonereach]].

## The toll-house

The [[greycloaks]] hold the Wallgate the way they hold everything: one house, one captain,
one open door. [[tolm]] sits in it. Nobody has ever taken the Wallgate by force, because
nobody has ever needed to. It is a gate that cannot be shut, and the order likes those.

## What comes through

Since the grey lifted, things come down out of the north through the Wallgate that did not
before. The toll-house tithes them like anyone else.
