## The fen

Where the rivers of the east come to the sea they give up and spread out into a hundred
miles of mangrove, reed and black water under a canopy that does not let the sun down.
Fever lives here, and leeches as long as a forearm, and things the fen-folk do not name.

## The temples

The [[ossar]] built in black stone, and here the stone still stands, stepped temples rising
out of the water with the jungle grown over them. The fen-folk fish around them and do not
go inside. The ones who do go in come out pale, if they come out.

## The fen-folk

Small, quiet, tattooed people who pole flat boats through the channels and trade with the
reavers of [[the-teeth]]. They worship nothing. They say worship is what the Ossar wanted.
