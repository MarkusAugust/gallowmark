## The villages

A drifthold is a raft of reed as big as a field, a dozen huts on it, gardens in baskets, and
the whole thing moving a little every day with the current. When the fen-folk want to go
somewhere else, they pole it. When something finds them, they cut the ropes and scatter.

## Why

The fen-folk say a place that stays still gets known, and a place that gets known gets visited,
and the [[ossar]] visit. No drifthold has ever been built within sight of [[the-black-step]].
