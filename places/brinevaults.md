## The vaults

Kell kept its salt, its coin and its records under the hall. In the long counting-rooms the
clerks copied the ledger [[linelord]] by lamplight. The copies are still on the shelves. The
last of them count days, not lines. When the sea came in the clerks barred the vault doors
with the coin inside and themselves with it, and the doors held, and the air held for a while.
A stroke for every day, in a hand that gets worse, until it stops.

## The ropes

Ropes hang down the cistern shafts, drawn taut toward the hall, and [[the-drawn]] move along
them. In the deepest cistern the water has dripped for forty years and left a column of salt as
thick as a man, and the clerks' coin is under it.
