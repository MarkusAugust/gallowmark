## The land

North of the [[wallgate]] the world goes white and stays white. Snowfields, black stone,
lakes frozen to the bottom, and wind that does not stop for a generation at a time. The
clans of the Rimewold live in longhouses dug into the hillsides and fight over the few
valleys where anything grows. [[brandfell]] smokes on the southern horizon, and since it
split the snow on that side melts in the middle of winter.

## The clans

The [[rimeclans]] have no king and want none. They raid south through the Wallgate when the
winters are bad, and the winters are always bad. A clan is a hall, a chief, and whoever
followed the chief home from the last fight.

## The ice

The ice is not only weather. In the deep north it stands in walls taller than any tower,
and in it, if you go far enough, there are shapes. The clans do not go that far.
