## The bottom

Under three men's depth of ash and the smoke that stands on it, the floor of [[greyhollow]] is
black stone, and on the stone stands a temple: a ring of [[ossar]] columns, too tall, around a
crack in the ground a hundred paces long. The crack goes down. Heat comes up out of it, and the
smoke, and a sound like breath.

## Why it was built

The Ossar built their temples near their masters. This one is the nearest any of them came to
the surface in the north: the place where [[the-unborn]] could be heard best. When the knot was
tied it was buried and forgotten. The ash of [[the-ashfall]] filled the hollow over it. The
smoke is what comes up through the ash.

## Who has been there

[[mardra]]. Some [[smokewalkers]]. Nobody else who came back.
