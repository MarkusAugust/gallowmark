## The rooms

Every vault of the clerks had its strongroom, behind a door with two locks and a
clerk's seal on each. The first ledgers were kept there, and the clerks' own
pay, and the things the clerks took in lieu. The doors held when the water came
in. The packing has made them part of the wall.
