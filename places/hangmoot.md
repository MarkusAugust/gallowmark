## The ridge

Nine gallows on a bare ridge, older than [[kell]], taller than anything else the first rulers
built. Eight of them are used. The ninth has never held anyone that anyone will name, and it
is the one the wind plays. This is where [[hask]] hangs, if he hangs anywhere.

## The Rope-Moot

Once a year, when the rivers are lowest, the Mark comes up the ridge. Debts are read out by
the Reeve. What can be paid is paid. What cannot is hanged or forgiven, and the Mark has
always found the first cheaper. The [[greycloaks]] attend, politely, and read the longest
list.

## The slack rope

The hangmen say one of the nine ropes has hung slack since [[the-drowning-of-kell]], and
that a slack rope is a debt nobody has collected. Since [[the-ebb]] the Reeve has had the
ninth gallows re-roped, and the Mark noticed.
