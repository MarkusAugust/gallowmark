## The realm

The army crowned [[marcus-halberd]], and the army is what he kept: the north road, the border
houses, and [[grimwatch]], which he took from the Greycloaks by paying them more. His successors
are the captains of [[the-unmourned]], each chosen by the company, each sworn on the rope.

## In the year 150

Poor, hard, and the best soldiers in Harth. The March fights Solmerine and the See for the
heartland, and sends companies south to the Ossar front for pay. It worships [[nevermourn]] above
the sun, and its kings do not weep.
