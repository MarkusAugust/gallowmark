## The city

Harrowmere is built on piles driven into a black lake, a thousand palaces and ten thousand
hovels joined by canals. It has no king. It has [[the-gilded]], the merchant princes, who
bought the debts of [[old-vahr]] the year after it burned and have been collecting on them
since. Every kingdom in the Sunder owes Harrowmere. Harrowmere owes nobody, and says so.

## What is for sale

Grain, iron, ships, slaves, poisons, offices in [[crownhold]], the names of the dead,
sorcerers by the season, and once, they say, a god's tooth. The slave market on the
Stairs of Weighing is the largest in the world. The [[greycloaks]] keep a chapterhouse on
the Grand Canal and pay no harbour tax.

## Under the water

The black lake has no bottom anyone has found. The princes sink their debtors in it. Things
come up sometimes that were not sunk.
