## The glass

For a day's walk in every direction from [[old-vahr]], the glass is white. Not green, not black:
white, smooth as ice, and at night it gives off a light like the moon through cloud, so that
the dark never quite comes. The [[the-hadd]] call it the place where night does not come, and
will not walk on it for any price.

## What walks it

Tracks cross the white glass that are not Qaddir, not horse, not man. Caravans that cut a corner
of it lose people in the night without a sound. [[qeth-ummar]]'s Paid walk it to and from his
palace and come to no harm, which tells you something.
