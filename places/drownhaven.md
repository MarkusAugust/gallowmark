## The place

Drownhaven is the largest fishing town of the Gallowmark coast, west of Wrackhead, where every family has lost someone to the sea and says so in the name. Its people fish, pay the salt-pile at [[wrackhead]] for [[vorr]], pay the
[[greycloaks]] their tenth, and trust neither.
