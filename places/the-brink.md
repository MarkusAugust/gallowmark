## The line

Since [[the-fraying]] the [[feverfen]] and the south of the [[glassands]] are Ossar country. The
Brink is where that country ends, for now: watchtowers on the hills above the fen, Vahrish forts
reoccupied on the edge of the glass, villages that are burned every few summers by one side or the
other. Soldiers say *going to the Brink* the way the Mark says *going under*.

## Who holds it

Everyone, in turns. [[the-sunward-see]] marches south every summer under the sun-banner. Free
companies of [[the-halberd-march]] hold forts for pay. [[the-saddle-throne]] rides along it and
kills what comes out. [[harrowmere]] pays for it, at interest. The [[greycloaks]] hold its gates,
and the gates are always open.

## What comes over it

[[the-blindkin]] in the night, [[the-paid]] in columns, [[seepers]] through the ground, and once or
twice a year a [[the-stonesleepers]] walking upright, and then a fort is gone.
