## The desert

South of Crownhold the land goes yellow, then white, then hard. For a hundred miles around
[[old-vahr]] the sand is glass, green and black and cracked, and a man who walks on it in
the noon sun goes blind. The caravans cross at night, between the old cities, carrying salt,
glass, and what the diggers bring out of the ruins.

## The ruins

Vahr's cities are still out there, half drowned in sand: towers, aqueducts, streets of
statues with their faces melted. Under them are older foundations, black and too large, that
were the [[ossar]]'s. Diggers go down for gold and come up with bones too long for any grave.

## The kings of the glass

In the old towers sit sorcerer-kings who rule a well, a ruin and a few hundred desperate
people each, and who make pacts with [[the-unborn]] the way other kings raise taxes. The
greatest of them is [[ommet]], in Old Vahr itself.
