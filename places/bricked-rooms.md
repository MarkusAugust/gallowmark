## The rooms

When the crown went empty and the knives came up the hill, the Lowstreets did
what streets do: they hid what they had. Families bricked up a back room with the silver in it and
lived in the front. Some bricked themselves in with it. The water came, and the
packing filled every crack, and the rooms are still shut from the inside.
