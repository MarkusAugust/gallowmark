## The realm

The chapel crowned [[seraphine-vasthorne]]. When she broke the ring, the chapel burned her, and the
Exarch [[hadrian-mournevale]] took the throne in the name of [[helion-castor]], for safekeeping.
Nobody has given it back. The See is the grain heartland of old [[crownhold]], ruled from the
cathedral by Exarchs of the [[the-sunward-faith]].

## In the year 150

The See is the shield against the dark: its armies march south to the Ossar front every summer
under the sun-banner. It is also the realm that burns the most heretics, sells the most offices,
and has the most silver-eyed bishops.
