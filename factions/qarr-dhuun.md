## The guild

Rope, lamp, pick and a debt to a Harrowmere house: that is a digger. Qarr-Dhuun licenses them,
maps the ruins, sells the finds, and takes half. They go down through the sand into Vahr's
streets, and under the streets into the black foundations of the [[ossar]].

## What they bring up

Gold, glass, statues with melted faces, and bones too long for any grave, which sell in
[[harrowmere]] for more than gold to buyers who do not give names. About one digger in five
does not come up. The guild writes them off in its ledger the same day.
