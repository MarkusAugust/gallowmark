## Who they are

Every slave sold in [[harrowmere]] is weighed against salt on the Stairs of Weighing, and the
salt is the price. The Saltless are the ones who ran, from galleys, mines and canal-houses, and
reached the mountains. They buried the salt they were bought for, and called the place
[[saltgrave]], and themselves the ones without a price.

## The law

No one in Saltgrave owns anyone. Anyone who tries is hanged in salt-sacks. Anyone who brings a
slaver's coin in is hanged beside them. Anyone who leaves and comes back is asked one question
at the gate, and nobody outside knows what it is.
