## Who they are

Small, hard, bow-legged, born in the saddle, and named for it. They have no cities and want none.
A Hoofborn host is a nation on horseback: men, women, children, herds, and the gods in carts.

## The coming

Once a generation the grass fails, or something east of the grass pushes them, and they come
west over the hills into [[crownhold]] and up to the lake of [[harrowmere]]. They last came sixty
years ago. They burned forty towns, took a king's head, and went home. The old men of Crownhold
remember the fires. The calendars of the Gilded say they are due again, and [[the-gilded]] have
begun buying horses.
