## The company

Four thousand foot, six hundred horse, a baggage train like a town, and a black banner with a
white crow. The Unmourned have fought for Crownhold, against it, and for Harrowmere twice. Since
their captain stopped hiding his face, they fight for him.

## Custom

They do not bury their dead. They lay them out for the crows on the field, and sing over them
loudly and badly, and march on in the morning. A man of the Unmourned who weeps is fined a
month's pay. Most of them think it is cheap.
