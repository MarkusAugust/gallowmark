## Who they are

*Hadd* is their word for the border, and they are the people of it: the line between glass and
sand, between night and day, between the ruins and the living. They walk only at night, faces
veiled to the eyes, and sleep under black tents through the burning day. A Hadd guide is the
only way across the [[glassands]] that is not a way to die.

## Names

Their names come from a language with no cousin in Harth, hard and full of breaks: Qeth,
Ysh'ra, Torr-Halaq, Ummar-Seb. A child is not named until it has walked one night on the
glass.

## Custom

To walk by day is to be a *sun-fool*. To unveil before a stranger is to offer your life. The
Hadd will guide anyone across the glass for water, and will leave anyone on it who lies to
them.
