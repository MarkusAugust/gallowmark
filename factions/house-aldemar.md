## The house

House Aldemar is one of the five great houses of [[crownhold]], the oldest house of Crownhold, holders of Granaria and the grain, who have married into the throne three times and never sat on it.

## In the Three Crowns

Every great house must choose in [[the-three-crowns]]. House Aldemar has chosen, or says it has, which in
[[solmerine]] is not the same thing.
