## The kings

Water is power in the [[glassands]], and every well outside [[ush-kaddah]] has a king. They
live in the old Vahrish towers above their wells, with the people who need the water living
below. Each is a sorcerer. Each has paid [[the-unborn]] in at least one coin, and most in two.

## Their wars

A Well-King does not raise an army. He asks. Wells go bitter, caravans lose their way, a rival's
people wake up in the night unable to remember their names. [[qeth-ummar]] is the greatest of
them, and the others pay him in water to be left alone.
