## The houses

Nine houses, each with a palace on the Grand Canal, a fleet, a bank and a feud with the other
eight. A prince is whoever holds the house's ledger. Princes rarely die of old age. [[aleid-van-der-haalst]],
of the House of Salt, has held hers for thirty years, which is the record.

## The debt

When [[old-vahr]] burned, the Gilded bought its debts from the dead for a copper on the
crown. Every kingdom of Harth grew up owing Harrowmere for an empire it never saw. The
princes do not collect it all. They collect enough.
