## Who they are

Most who go into the smoke of [[greyhollow]] do not come out. Those who do are Smokewalkers. Their
eyes catch firelight a moment too long. They are warm to the touch in winter.

## What they come out with

A gift, sometimes: knowing where fire is under the ground, walking through flame unburned, being
believed. A hunger, sometimes: for heat, for meat, for the smoke itself, until they walk back in
and do not come out again. Nobody knows which they will get until they get it.

## In the world

- [[the-kneeling]] at [[lowfold]] send their own into the smoke on purpose.
- [[the-ashen]] do not count Smokewalkers among them, and will not say why.
