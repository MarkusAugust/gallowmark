## The house

House Corvaith is one of the five great houses of [[crownhold]], the soldier-house of the Eastmarch, which leads the Iron Hundred and has not forgiven the Hoofborn for sixty years.

## In the Three Crowns

Every great house must choose in [[the-three-crowns]]. House Corvaith has chosen, or says it has, which in
[[solmerine]] is not the same thing.
