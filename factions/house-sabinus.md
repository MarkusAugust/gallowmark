## The house

House Sabinus is one of the five great houses of [[crownhold]], the money-house of the Saltcoast, deepest in debt to Harrowmere and deepest in its counting-houses, which backs Tiberian the Fair.

## In the Three Crowns

Every great house must choose in [[the-three-crowns]]. House Sabinus has chosen, or says it has, which in
[[solmerine]] is not the same thing.
