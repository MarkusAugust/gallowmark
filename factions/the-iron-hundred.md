## The order

They were the Iron Thousand once, the heaviest horse in Harth, and they rode out to meet
[[the-hoofborn]] sixty years ago in [[marcheast]] and were shot to pieces in a single afternoon by
riders who never let them close. A hundred came home. The order kept the number as its name and
swore a [[rope-oaths]] to be a thousand again before it dies.

## Now

They are a hundred still, led by [[house-corvaith]], heavy, proud and obsolete, and they are the
only part of Crownhold's army that wants [[sorgan-windbreaker]] to come.
