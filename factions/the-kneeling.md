## What they are

Not one church. Cells: a cellar in [[crownhold]], a barge in [[harrowmere]], a ruin in the
[[glassands]], a farm at the edge of [[greyhollow]]. They meet at night, they kneel with
their foreheads on bare earth, and they ask. Sometimes they are answered.

## What they believe

That the [[the-first-knot]] was a crime. That men were happier counted. That the knot is
loosening, and that those who knelt first will be eaten last.

## How they work

They cut ropes. A gallows burned, a debt forgiven, an oath broken in public, an iron ring
stolen: the Kneeling do these things, because each one loosens the knot a little. The
hangmen of [[hangmoot]] hunt them, and hang them, and say each hanging pulls it tight again.
