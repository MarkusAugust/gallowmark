## What they were

Taller than a man by a head, white as a thing grown in a cellar, with eyes like wet stone
and blood that does not warm. The Ossar built the black temple-cities whose roots are still
under the [[feverfen]] and the [[glassands]], and in them they kept the [[the-unborn]]
company and kept men the way men keep pigs: fed, counted, and taken.

They taught men sorcery. It was the cruellest thing they did, because it worked.

## The fall

Men rose, once, and lost, many times. Then they tied the [[the-first-knot]] on a ridge in
the north, and the oath held, and the Ossar went down into the earth after their masters.
Not all of them. Not all the way.

## Now

Bones too long for any grave are dug up in the Glassands every year. The fen-folk of the
Feverfen say the Ossar never left their temples, only the daylight. The [[the-kneeling]] say
the Ossar are coming back, and that it will be good to be counted again.
