## What they are

The Greycloaks began as the men who guarded the drowned quays of [[kell]] so that nobody
would steal what the sea had not yet taken. Forty years on, they guard everything, and what
they guard they tithe. Their cloaks are grey because grey hides ash, and the order was born
in the [[the-ashfall]].

## How they take a village

Never by storm. A Greycloak captain rides in alone, finds the one gate left open, and sits
down inside it. By evening the village is paying tithe and thinks it decided to. Every siege
in Gallowmark has been lost this way, which is why the wise close every gate, including the
ones they cannot see.

## The books

What the order tithes it writes down. The tithe-books hang from the hooks on the bridge at
[[hookford]], in Captain [[drusk]]'s hand, and the hangmen say they are a poor copy of the
ledger [[linelord]]. Captain [[tolm]] keeps the [[wallgate]] and writes nothing down.

## Beyond the Mark

The order holds Gallowmark, and nothing else, and is everywhere. It keeps a chapterhouse in
every city of [[harth]]: on the Grand Canal in [[harrowmere]], under the palace walls of
[[crownhold]], at the wells of the [[glassands]]. It rules no kingdom and wants none. It is
hired: to farm a king's taxes, keep a city's gates, guard a prince's debtors, hold a pass.
It is paid a tenth, and it takes the tenth politely, and somewhere in every kingdom there is
a gate the order holds and the king has forgotten he gave it.

The order is older than it says. It is [[the-tenth-legion]] of Vahr, which kept its gates when
the empire burned and never stopped. Nobody knows who leads it. The captains answer to the books, and the books answer to
someone.

## Enemies


[[gorvek]] has a price on his head that the order raises every winter. [[sarn]] they have tried
to hire and to kill, and the order's own records disagree on which came first.
