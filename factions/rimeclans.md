## Who they are

Tall, pale-haired, cold-eyed, and loud, which is how the Reach can tell them from its own.
A clan is a longhouse, a chief, and the men and women who followed the chief home. They count
wealth in iron, horses and the skulls over the door.

## What they want

Warm land. There is none north of the Wallgate, and the [[greycloaks]] hold the gate, so
every bad winter the clans come down to the toll-house and [[tolm]] asks them politely for a
tenth, and every bad winter some of them pay and some of them do not.

## Custom

A clan-chief is chosen by the clan standing behind them. When nobody stands behind, there
is no chief. [[hrok]] has had the longest line behind him in living memory.
