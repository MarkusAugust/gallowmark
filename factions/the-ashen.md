## Who they are

When the ash came down, most of the east fled south. The Ashen did not. They stayed in their
villages on the rim of [[greyhollow]], ate what they could, breathed what they had to, and came
out of the generation grey to the bone. Their children are born grey. They tattoo themselves
with ash rubbed into cuts, a line for every winter they have outlasted.

## What the Mark thinks

That they eat their dead. That [[mardra]] is one of them. That they can walk into the smoke of
the Hollow and out again. Two of those are true.
