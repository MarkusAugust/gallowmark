## The house

House Trevane is one of the five great houses of [[crownhold]], the border house of the north road, holders of the march toward Gallowmark and Grimwatch, hard, poor and proud.

## In the Three Crowns

Every great house must choose in [[the-three-crowns]]. House Trevane has chosen, or says it has, which in
[[solmerine]] is not the same thing.
