## The crews

Every one of them has rowed in chains. They wear the scar of the oar-iron on the ankle and do
not hide it. They take only slave ships, or ships that owe [[the-gilded]], which is all of them.

## Custom

They leave a galley drifting with its hold open and its masters chained to the oars. The
[[the-saltless]] of [[saltgrave]] send them salt every year, which in Saltgrave is the
greatest honour there is.
