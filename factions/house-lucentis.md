## The house

House Lucentis is one of the five great houses of [[crownhold]], the house of bishops and scholars, which fills half the high offices of the Sunward Faith and backs Seraphine in public.

## In the Three Crowns

Every great house must choose in [[the-three-crowns]]. House Lucentis has chosen, or says it has, which in
[[solmerine]] is not the same thing.
