## The company

A guildhall with a sign over the door, a charter with nine forged seals, a tariff of fees
painted on the wall: so much for a purse, so much for a ring, so much for a man's papers out
of a locked room. A member pays dues. A non-member who steals in Harrowmere is found in the
canal. The city watch is a member.

## Its founder

The Company says [[sarn]] wrote its charter, a long time before Kell, and left it unsigned.
The charter is in the guildhall under glass. The bottom of it is blank. Every master of the
Company has looked at that blank and decided not to fill it in.
