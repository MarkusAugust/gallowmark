## Who they were

Clan Skoll was a small, hard clan of the far north, sixty spears and a longhouse. When
[[brandfell]] split and the winters went strange, Skoll and two other clans walked north into
the ice toward [[the-sleepers-wall]], looking for land the fire could not reach.

## Who came back

Skoll came back. Fewer, thinner, and pale to the lips. They do not light fires, not for cooking,
not for warmth, and they sit in the snow at night without cloaks. They say they made a bargain
at the Wall. They will not say with whom. The other two clans did not come back at all.
