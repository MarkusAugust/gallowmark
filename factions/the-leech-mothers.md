## Who they are

Old women with leech-jars at their belts and black stains to the elbow. They draw the fever
out with leeches, kill the leeches, and burn them. They know every poison in the
[[feverfen]] and use most of them as medicine.

## What they know

The fever comes from the temples, they say. To learn to cure it they went in. Every Leech-Mother
has been inside [[the-black-step]] at least once, as her last lesson. Some do not come out.
Those who do know more about the [[ossar]] than any scholar of [[crownhold]], and say very
little.
