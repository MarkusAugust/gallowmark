## The order

The Reeve of [[hangmoot]] and the hangmen under him. Every one of them has been hanged, once, as
their oath, and cut down before the end; they wear the scar like a collar. They know what
[[the-first-knot]] is. That is their whole secret, and they keep it.

## The work

They hang, on the nine gallows and wherever else they are needed, because every debt paid at
the rope pulls the knot tight. They hunt [[the-kneeling]] from Gallowmark to [[harrowmere]], and
burn [[the-paid]], because a rope does not hold what has no breath.
