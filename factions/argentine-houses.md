## Who they are

A dozen houses, more or less: three in [[crownhold]], two in [[harrowmere]], one with a ruin in
the [[glassands]], one in Gallowmark that does not admit it. Every few generations a child is
born to them with eyes like new silver, and that child inherits, whatever the order of birth.
They say the eyes are the blood of [[old-vahr]].

## The whisper

The emperors of Vahr learned their sorcery from the [[ossar]], and the Ossar's eyes were pale.
The Argentine Houses are fond of the cold, slow to warm, and very good at sorcery. They marry
each other. They do not let physicians bleed them.
