## The lords

After [[the-ashfall]] the Mark was empty of everything but the [[greycloaks]] and whoever could
hold a hill. The Holdfast Lords are whoever held one: a tower on a mound, a ditch, a palisade,
a hall full of hired swords. They call themselves lords. The Greycloaks call them that too,
politely, and take a tenth of whatever they take.

## Their wars

Small, endless and bloody. A holdfast changes hands every few winters. The lords hire Ashborn
out of [[bonereach]] for the fighting, and the Ashborn hire out to whoever is winning.
