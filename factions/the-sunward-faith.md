## The church

Every town in [[crownhold]] has a sun-temple with a gilt dome, and every temple takes its tithe,
and the tithes go up to the cathedral in the capital, where the Exarch of the Sunward sits beside
the throne of [[aurelian-vasthorne]]. Offices in the church are bought the way offices at court
are. Many priests are believers. Few bishops are.

## Its enemies

The faith hates [[the-kneeling]] by doctrine: below against above. It also hates [[the-ninth-rope]],
because the hangmen say the sun is not what holds the dark down, a rope is. And it fears
[[quintus-ashhand]], who was one of its priests, and burned his arm in one of its chapels.
