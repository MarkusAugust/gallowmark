## The legion

Every gate of Vahr had a cohort of the Tenth, grey-cloaked, polite, taking a tenth. The legion
was never sent to war. It did not need to be: it held the gates, and an army that does not own
the gates is just a crowd.

## After the fall

When [[old-vahr]] burned, the Tenth did not. It had no emperor to obey and kept its gates anyway,
for whoever paid. In the [[the-broken-years]] it forgot its name, kept its cloaks and its tenth,
and became the order. The [[greycloaks]] say they began guarding the quays of [[kell]]. They began
there again. They are much older than that.
