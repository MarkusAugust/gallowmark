## Who they are

Barefoot, wet to the knee, and unwelcome at most tables, the river-priests walk the banks of
the grey rivers of [[gallowmark]] and keep the names of things. They hold that every stream
in the Mark is the same river, [[thurn]], pretending to be many, and that to name a thing
truly is to have a grip on it.

## The naming

It was the river-priests who named [[sarn]] the Lord of Streams, and they meant it as a
warning: here is the thing that goes where water goes. They also named the artifact that
[[gorvek]] carries. They called it [[streamlord]], and then they stopped talking about it.

## Custom

A river-priest will answer any question with a name, and never with a reason.
