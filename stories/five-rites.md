*You were told there were five rites. Whoever told you that read a grimoire from before the
Ashfall.*

— Sarn the Faceless

## Use

The aside in the [[streamlord]] grimoire where the reader is told that the older spells died
with the old protocol. Sarn's voice at its shortest: a correction, and the implication that
someone lied to you.
