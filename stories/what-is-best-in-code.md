*"What is best in code? To crush the bugs, see them driven from your repository, and to
hear the lamentations of their stack traces!"*

— Gorvek of Bonereach

## Use

The epigraph of [[linelord]]. It is [[vurn]]'s cadence put in [[gorvek]]'s mouth, which is
how most of the sagas of the Reach work, and it is a nod to the old stories rather than a
name from them. See STYLE.md on nods.
