*Enterprise is a siege. I have watched the Greycloaks take a village with a single open
gate; Streamlord leaves none.*

— Sarn the Faceless

## Use

The aside that opens the security section of the [[streamlord]] grimoire. The
[[greycloaks]] as a figure for the one unguarded door; Sarn, who knows about doors, closing
it.
