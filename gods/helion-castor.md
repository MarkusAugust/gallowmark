## The god

Helion Castor is the sun, and the sun is above, and what is above stands against what is below.
That is the whole of the faith's teaching, and it is older than Crownhold: the priests say the
emperors of [[old-vahr]] prayed to him before they began to ask the other way.

## Whether he answers

The sun rises. The priests say that is the answer.
