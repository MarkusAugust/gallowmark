## The god

[[thurn]] is what flows. Vorr is where it stops. The [[river-priests]] say every grey river
is one river; the coast folk say yes, and it ends in Vorr's mouth. Vorr is the salt at the
bottom of things, the weight in a drowned man's boots, the god of what is kept. The people
of the drowned coast do not pray to Vorr. They pay. A plank from every wreck goes onto the
salt-pile at [[wrackhead]], and nobody says what for.

## Kell

Vorr took [[kell]] in one night and has been chewing since. The coast folk hold that the
crown never held the rivers; it held Vorr's mouth shut, and [[sarn]] opened it. The
river-priests call this a coast story. They have never gone down to the salt-pile to say so.

## The Ebb

Since [[the-ebb]] the sea has been going out from Old Kell. The coast folk say Vorr is
opening his mouth to give something back, and that a god who has kept for forty years does
not give back for nothing.
