## The goddess

The outsiders call her Mother Murk. The fen-folk do not call her anything when she can hear.
She is the fen itself: the warm black water, the roots, the rot that makes the fen green. She
gives fish, reed, children and fever, and she takes what falls in.

## Feeding

The fen-folk say they worship nothing, because worship is what the [[ossar]] wanted. But every
village drops the first fish of the day back into the water, and the old people go into it
when they are done, without a boat, singing.
