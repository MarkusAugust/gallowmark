## The god

The clans of the [[rimewold]] do not say his name. They are not sure he has one. They say
*the Old Smith*, and look south toward [[brandfell]] when they say it. Under the snow, under
the mountain, he works. The ring of his hammer is the ground shaking. The ash that came down
over the north was the sweepings of his floor.

## What he wants

Something burned every winter: a ship, a hall, a horse, a man. Smiths and charcoal-burners
pray to him, and every forge in the north has a hole in the back wall where the fire can see
out.

## In the world

- Since Brandfell split, the clans say, he has been forging something big. [[mardra]] says the
  clans are right that something is down there, and wrong about what.
