## The goddess

Nevermourn walks every field after the fighting, at dusk, in a cloak of black feathers, and
the crows come down where she points. She does not weep. That is the whole of her teaching:
the dead are not lost, they are given, and she takes them, and nothing given to her is ever
wasted.

## Her people

Sellswords, camp-followers, the gravediggers of every army, and the hangmen's crows. A free
company gives her one eye before a great battle, cut out of the head of the first enemy taken
alive. Soldiers do not say *he died*. They say *she has him*.

## Custom

To mourn openly for a soldier is to rob her. Free companies sing on the night after a battle,
loudly and badly, and anyone who weeps is put out of the firelight until they stop.
