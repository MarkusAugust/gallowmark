## What they are

Nobody living has seen one, and the [[ossar]], who served them, did not have a word for them
that a human mouth can make. They are older than [[thurn]], older than the sea [[vorr]]
keeps, older than the first rope. They were not born, so they do not die. They wait.

They are under the world: under the ice of the [[rimewold]], under the glass of the
[[glassands]], under the fens and the mountains and the drowned coast. The [[the-first-knot]]
put them there. It did not put them anywhere they cannot be heard.

## Sorcery

All sorcery is a pact with something below. A sorcerer asks, the Unborn give, and the
price is taken from the body, the memory or the soul, in that order, and the asking gets
easier each time. That is how every sorcerer in the Sunder ends: easier and easier, and then
not a person. [[mardra]] says she has paid only in the first two coins. Nobody believes her.

## Stirring

The knot is loosening. A rope went slack the night [[sarn]] emptied the
[[iron-crown-of-kell]], and ten years later [[brandfell]] split and burned. The fire under
the Mark, the warm iron, the rising coast: the river-priests have names for all of it. The
[[the-kneeling]] have one name for all of it, and they say it on their knees.
