## The god

[[gallowmark]] is named for the gallows, and the gallows are for Hask. The first rulers of
the Mark, before [[kell]] had a quay, built nine gallows on a ridge and hanged their god on
the ninth so the land would hold. That is the story the hangmen tell, and they tell it with
the knot already tied. Hask did not die. A hanged god does not; he hangs. Everything owed in
the Mark is owed to the rope, and the rope is his.

## The debt

Hask is the god of what is owed. A tithe is a debt, an oath is a debt, a name given is a
debt. The [[greycloaks]] have never written Hask's name in a ledger, and they collect in his
shape: patiently, and once. Hask has no priests. He has hangmen, and the Reeve of
[[hangmoot]] is the first among them.

## The rope and the crown

The hangmen say the land is held in place by nine ropes and one iron ring, and that the night
[[sarn]] emptied the [[iron-crown-of-kell]] one of the nine went slack. They do not say which.
Since [[the-ebb]] they have stopped saying nothing.
