## The god

The people of [[ysveld]] say there is something above as there is something below. [[the-unborn]]
were before birth, under the world. Aelarion is between the stars, over it: cold, bright, patient,
watching. It is not kind. It does not want what the Unborn want. That is the whole of its goodness.

## Its servants

Ysveld serves Aelarion as the [[ossar]] served the Unborn: utterly. Its envoys in Harth, like
[[saelith]], are its eyes. They buy Ossar bones to learn the enemy. They ask about [[the-nine-irons]]
because the knot is the front line, and Ysveld wants to know how long it will hold.
