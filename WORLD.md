# Gallowmark

A march of gallows and grey rivers between the Wallmountains and a drowned coast. Forty
years before the calendar starts, a faceless thief took three rubies from an iron crown and a
city went under the sea. Then the grey came down over the north and stayed a generation.
The people who walked out of it are the people of this world: short on words, long on
memory, and unwilling to trust anything they cannot hold.

## The pitch in one breath

Grimdark without the cynicism. Gallowmark is brutal, but its people are not; they are tired,
stubborn and occasionally magnificent. There is one god who does not care, one order that
takes villages through the gate you forgot, one thief who narrates everything, and one
barbarian who wants only that the stream obeys.

## The theme

Things held in place by belief are held in place by nothing. Streams obey the one who means
it. Names have teeth.

## What Gallowmark is not
