## What is true

This entry is the truth behind the descent, for the people who write it. No character in
the Mark says it plainly, and no line in the game states it. The player reads it in what
they find.

Ten years after [[sarn]] took the stones and [[kell]] went under, the mountain the Reach
calls [[brandfell]] split open north of the [[wallgate]], and ash came down over the north
for a generation. That was [[the-ashfall]]. It was fire, and fire does not go away when the
sky clears. It went under.

It runs under the whole Mark now, from Brandfell to the coast, close under [[greyhollow]]
where the ash never cooled, and closest of all under [[kell]]. It is lifting the ground the
way a loaf lifts in the oven. The sea is not going out. The coast is coming up out of it.
That is [[the-ebb]]. [[thurn]] calling the rivers home, [[vorr]] opening his mouth, [[hask]]'s
rope drawn tight: all three are given the credit, and none of them is the reason.

## What the fire is

[[mardra]] says the fire under the Mark is not a fire. It is [[the-unborn]], turning over in
their sleep, because the knot is looser than it was. She charges a great deal for saying so,
and she does not ask anyone to believe her.

## Why the iron is warm

[[rauk]] touched the crown and said the iron was warm. The first river runs warm one night
each winter at the Wallgate. The floor of [[the-iron-hall]] is warm under the salt. Every
one of them is the same fire, nearer the top than anyone has guessed.

## Why everyone goes down

Not for the truth. For what Kell had: the coin in the [[brinevaults]], the goods in the
[[bonded-stores]], the silver in the [[bricked-rooms]], the empty [[iron-crown-of-kell]] on
a dead king's head, and the rumour, which Sarn has never denied, that one of the three rubies
never left the hall. The ledger names whoever holds the most of it. That is the whole
descent: a drowned city, uncovered, and everyone in the Mark with a rope and a knife.

## What the player learns, tier by tier

1. [[quaysteps]]: ash in the silt that the rivers carried down; the order's first men, dead
   at their posts, still on duty.
2. [[lowstreets]]: the doors were barred against the city, not the water.
3. [[brinevaults]]: the clerks shut themselves in with the coin and counted the days.
4. [[the-iron-hall]]: the king, his guard still standing, a warm floor, and the stair he
   walled up behind him.
