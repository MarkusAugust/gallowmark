## The night

[[sarn]] entered the iron-roofed hall of [[kell]], took the three rubies from the
[[iron-crown-of-kell]], and was gone. Nobody saw a face. By morning the grey rivers had left
their beds and the quays were under water.

## What it made

The guards of the drowned quays became the [[greycloaks]]. The river-priests of [[thurn]]
gave Sarn the name Lord of Streams that same year, as a warning. And the Mark learned the
lesson it has lived by since: a thing held in place by belief is held in place by nothing.
