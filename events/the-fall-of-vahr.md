## The empire

Vahr was the first thing men built that the [[ossar]] had not. It ran from the ice to the
fens, it wore iron crowns because iron was the metal of the knot, and for a thousand years it
kept the oath and hanged its debtors on Hask's pattern. Then it began to use what the Ossar
had taught.

## The asking

The emperors of [[old-vahr]] asked [[the-unborn]] for victories, and got them; for long
lives, and got them; and at the last, for an empire that would never end. Something gave
it to them. The heartland burned in one night, white, and the sand around the capital ran
like water and set like glass. That is the [[glassands]].

## What was left

The provinces woke up without an emperor and became kingdoms: [[crownhold]], which says it
is Vahr's heir; [[harrowmere]], which bought the empire's debts and calls that the same
thing; the cities of the coast, of which [[kell]] kept the ninth of [[the-nine-irons]]. The rest of the
[[harth]] is what happens when nobody holds a land for a thousand years.
