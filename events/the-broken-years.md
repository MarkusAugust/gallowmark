## What happened

Everything, and none of it for long. A hundred kingdoms claimed the empire and ninety-eight
fell. [[crownhold]] lasted. [[harrowmere]] bought the debts. The Red Cough emptied the river
cities twice. The [[the-hoofborn]] came off [[the-grass-sea]] four times. [[the-kneeling]] took
a city and the hangmen burned it: [[the-burning-of-ossery]].

## What it made

The Harth of now: kingdoms that remember an empire, an order in grey that holds every gate, a
city that owns everyone's debt, and a knot that nobody but the hangmen remembers is there.
