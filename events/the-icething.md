## The moot

Every seventh winter the [[rimeclans]] walk out onto the great frozen lake of the middle
Rimewold and pitch their tents on the ice. For nine days nobody may draw blood except in the
ring. Feuds are fought out with fists and axe-hafts, marriages made, marriages broken, chiefs
counted by who stands behind them. [[hrok]] has been counted the longest line at five
Icethings running.

## The law of the ice

Blood on the ice outside the ring breaks the truce, and the clan that broke it is put off the
lake into the night with the [[rimehounds]]. It has happened twice in living memory.
