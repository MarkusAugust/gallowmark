## What is happening

In the first winter after the grey lifted, the sea went out from Old [[kell]] on the low
tide and did not come all the way back. Every month since, it has gone a little further. The
quay-stones of the drowned city are showing below [[wrackhead]]. On a still day the iron
roof of the hall stands clear of the water for an hour. Nobody has walked to it yet.
Everybody is going to.

## Three gods, one name

[[vell]] named it the Ebb before the coast had noticed, and the [[river-priests]] say
[[thurn]] is calling the rivers home to their beds. The coast folk say [[vorr]] is opening
his mouth to give something back. The hangmen of [[hangmoot]] say a rope that went slack
forty-one years ago is being drawn tight from the other end, and that [[hask]] does not draw
a rope for nothing. All three are pointing at the same iron hall and the three empty sockets
in it.

## Who is moving

- The [[greycloaks]] said for forty years they would raise Kell. The sea is doing it for
  them. They have doubled the fort at Wrackhead and sent [[tolm]] south from the
  [[wallgate]] for the first time since the grey.
- [[gorvek]] has been told by Vell what he carries and has turned toward the coast. He has
  not said why.
- [[mardra]] has begun selling things from the grey that she says came up out of Kell.
- [[rauk]] has stopped diving, and [[brim]] walks to the waterline every morning.
- [[sarn]] narrates. Sarn always did. Sarn has started narrating this.

## What it binds

Every gallows, every river, every fort and every debt in the Mark is drawn tight toward one
iron hall standing up out of the salt. The [[iron-crown-of-kell]] held the world together by
being believed in. The sea is about to show everyone whether it is still there.
