## The shadow war

[[aurelian-vasthorne]] has outlived four heirs. Three are left, and each has begun the war
already: poison in the wine, fires in the granaries, councillors found in the river.
[[tiberian-vasthorne]] has the crowds and Harrowmere's money. [[seraphine-vasthorne]] has the
cellars and whatever answers her there. [[marcus-halberd]] has [[the-unmourned]].

## The winter he dies

The chronicles name it for what happens then: three crownings, in three cities, in one
winter. The capital crowns the Fair. The chapel crowns the Pale. The army crowns the Bastard.
