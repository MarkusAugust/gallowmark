## The rising

It started in a single village, when [[quintus-ashhand]] put the tax-rolls into the chapel
fire. Now it is nine provinces. The rebels march from village to village, open the granaries,
burn every list of who owes what, and pull down the [[greycloaks]]' chapterhouses stone by
stone.

## Why it frightens everyone

Not the peasants. The lists. In Harth a debt is a rope, and a rope is the knot, and the
hangmen of [[hangmoot]] have sent word south that every roll burned loosens it. The
[[the-kneeling]] have sent word too. They are delighted.
