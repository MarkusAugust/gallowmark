## The war

Harrowmere names it: Crownhold did not pay, so now it pays in blood. Whichever crown sits in
the capital, the debt sits under it, and the only way to be rid of a debt to a city with no
army is to burn the city. [[the-gilded]] know this, and have hired every free company in
Harth that is not already the [[the-unmourned]].

## Where it is fought

On the imperial road, in the river cities, and on the black lake, where most of the fallen go.
The Greycloaks are paid by both sides and fight for neither.
