## The night

[[aurelian-vasthorne]] died in the sixth winter after the ash cleared, and [[the-three-crowns]]
were crowned in three cities. In the ninth, [[seraphine-vasthorne]], crowned by the chapel, took
her father's circlet, one of [[the-nine-irons]], down into the cellars of [[solmerine]] and broke it
on the floor where she knelt. On [[hangmoot]], that same night, a rope snapped. [[haldis-graymane]]
heard it from her bed.

## What woke

In the [[feverfen]] the lights on [[the-black-step]] burned all night, and in the morning the
[[the-stonesleepers]] were walking. In the south of the [[glassands]] the foundations under the
ruins opened. Within a year both lands were lost: their people fled, were taken, or were counted.
[[smokemouth]] finished what was being built on it.

## After

The Sunward church that crowned Seraphine burned her in the cathedral square of what became
[[the-sunward-see]], and kept her throne. The other ropes held. They have held, barely, for a
hundred and forty years.

## A hundred and fifty years on

This is the Harth of the year 150:

- Crownhold is three kingdoms at war with each other: [[realm-of-solmerine]], [[the-sunward-see]]
  and [[the-halberd-march]]. Its east is [[the-saddle-throne]] of the Hoofborn.
- The Feverfen and the south of the Glassands are Ossar country. The front is a line of forts,
  ruins and burned villages, and nobody holds it for long.
- [[the-ninth-rope]] holds Hangmoot with eight ropes, and hunts.
- The [[greycloaks]] hold every gate, still, politely. They have never shut one.
- [[harrowmere]] is richer than ever. Everyone owes it for the war.
- Gorvek is a legend. Hookbreakers hang in every hall.
