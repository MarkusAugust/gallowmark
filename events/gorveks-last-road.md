## The road

Thirty-one years after the ash cleared, [[gorvek]] came through the [[wallgate]] going north.
[[tolm]] was in the door, old, and asked him for a tenth, and Gorvek put something in his
hand, and Tolm let him through and never said what it was. He came to [[hrok]]'s hall, asked
for a horse, got two, and rode north past [[brandfell]] into the white.

He had the sack from [[kell]] with him. Nobody saw what was in it.

## After

Nobody saw him again. That has not stopped anyone. The Reach says he went to finish
something. The [[rimeclans]] say he walked into the ice. The [[the-kneeling]] say he went to
cut Hask down. Every bastard line in the north says it is his, and some of them may be right.
