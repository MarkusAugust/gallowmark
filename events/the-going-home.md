## What is true

This entry is the truth behind the descent, for the people who write it. No character in
the Mark says it, and no line in the game states it. The player reads it in what they find.

When [[sarn]] took the stones, the three rivers left their beds, the sea came in over
[[kell]], and the place under the iron hall where the one river ends was opened. The grey
came up out of it, through the water, and climbed the rivers against the current to where
they rise beyond the [[wallgate]]. It came down over the north from there, and so the north
has always believed it came from the north. That was [[the-ashfall]].

When it lifted it did not go away. It went home, down the rivers, into the drain under the
hall, and it is drawing the sea after it. That is [[the-ebb]]. [[thurn]] calling the rivers
home, [[vorr]] opening his mouth, [[hask]]'s rope drawn tight: all three are right, and none
of them is the reason. [[greyhollow]] is the part that never made it home, and it rises when
the sea goes out because it is the same body.

[[vell]] told [[gorvek]] what he carries: the one thing that can tell a stream to stop. He
went down before anyone, and he is standing in the drain with [[streamlord]], holding it.

## What the player learns, tier by tier

1. [[quaysteps]]: ash in the silt; the order's first men, dead in the grey, still on duty.
2. [[lowstreets]]: the doors were barred against the grey, not the water.
3. [[brinevaults]]: the clerks counted the days of the grey; a fall of water stands still.
4. [[the-iron-hall]]: the grey at home, and Gorvek in the drain, and his line in the ledger.
