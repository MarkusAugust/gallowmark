## The auction

Once a year, at midwinter, the sorcerers of Harth who will work for coin come to
[[harrowmere]] and stand on the Stairs of Weighing in white masks, and the princes, kings and
captains of Harth bid for a year of each. The bidding is in the open. The contracts are not.

## The festival

The whole city goes masked. There is fire on the canals and music on every bridge, and the
[[the-honest-company-of-takers]] work all night with the city's blessing. It is also the night
the barges go to [[the-tenth-palace]].
