## The peace

Not peace: quiet. The emperors of [[old-vahr]] held Harth with roads, law and iron. Every debt was
written, every oath sworn on the rope, every gate held by [[the-tenth-legion]]. The roads are still
there, and the gallows by the milestones.

## The rot

For the first five centuries the emperors kept the [[the-nine-irons]] on their heads and the knot
tight. For the second five they read [[the-hungry-books]]. The peace ended the way it was always
going to.
