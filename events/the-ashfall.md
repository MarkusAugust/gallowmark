## What fell

Ten years after [[kell]] went under, [[brandfell]] split open in the Wallmountains, and the
fire in it came out. The sky over the north of [[gallowmark]] went black. Then the ash came
down: hot at first, burning thatch and birch and the people who could not get under stone,
then cold and grey and endless, a finger deep, a hand deep, to the knee. It fell on
[[bonereach]] first and hardest, and it stayed a generation. The sun was a coin behind it.
Nothing grew. Inside the dark people did what people do when nobody can see them.

The rivers carried it south. For thirty years the grey rivers ran grey in earnest, and laid
the ash down on the sea floor over drowned Kell, which is why the silt on the
[[quaysteps]] is not sand.

## What came out

When the sky cleared, the ones who walked out of the north were called the Ashborn.
[[gorvek]] is one. The [[greycloaks]] are, in a sense, another: the order was born guarding
the drowned quays of [[kell]], took the north in the dark years while nobody could see it
happen, and never took the cloak off.

## The calendar

The Mark counts its years from the clearing. Everything before is *in the grey*, and nobody
is precise about it.
