## What fell

Nobody agrees on what the Ashfall was: ash, mist, or something that wanted to be mistaken
for both. It came down over [[bonereach]] and the north of [[gallowmark]] and stayed for a
generation, and inside it people did what people do when nobody can see them.

## What came out

When it lifted, the ones who walked out were called the Ashborn. [[gorvek]] is one. The
[[greycloaks]] are, in a sense, another: the order was born guarding the drowned quays of
[[kell]] in the grey, and it never took the cloak off.

## The calendar

The Mark counts its years from the lifting. Everything before is *in the grey*, and nobody
is precise about it.
