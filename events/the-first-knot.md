## The rope

The first men had one god of their own, the god of oaths, and they had lost every war
against the [[ossar]]. So they swore the one oath that could not be broken, and they made a
god hold it: they built nine gallows on the ridge that is now [[hangmoot]] and hanged
[[hask]] on the ninth. A hanged god does not die. He hangs, and the oath hangs with him, and
what it binds stays bound. The Ossar and their masters went under.

## The ropes and the ring

The hangmen say the binding is nine ropes and one iron ring, and that the ring was carried
south and set with stones and worn by kings, until it was the [[iron-crown-of-kell]]. The
crown held the rivers in their beds because it held a great deal more than rivers.

## Why the Mark is the Mark

Everything owed in [[gallowmark]] is owed to the rope, because every debt paid at Hask's
gallows pulls the knot a little tighter. The Mark is not a borderland by accident. It is
the place where the world is tied shut.
