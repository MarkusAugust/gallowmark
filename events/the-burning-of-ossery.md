## The war

For a generation the Kneeling had been in Ossery's cellars. Then they were in its council, and then
its gates closed. What happened inside for the next year nobody wrote down. The river below the city
ran warm.

## The burning

[[the-ninth-rope]] came south with every hangman in Harth, and three kings came with their armies,
and they did not besiege Ossery. They burned it, all of it, with everyone inside, and hanged anyone
who came out. The hangmen say the knot was tighter afterward. The kings did not ask how.
