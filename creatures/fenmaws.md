## What

A Fenmaw is twice a boat's length, mostly jaw, and lies just under the surface of the
[[feverfen]]'s channels. When its mouth opens, there is a face on the tongue, soft and pale, and
it talks. It uses voices it has eaten. Fen-folk children are taught never to answer a voice
from the water, even their mother's.
