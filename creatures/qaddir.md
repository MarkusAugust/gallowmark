## What

The Hadd name means *those who listen for warmth*. Qaddir lie under the glass-crust in the
day, cool and still, and come up at night toward anything warm: a fire, a horse, a body. Their
scales are glass, grown from the sand they swallow, and a Qaddir hide is worth a season's water
in [[harrowmere]].

## In the world

- The [[the-hadd]] travel cold. No fires on the glass. The Qaddir are why.
- The big ones live near [[old-vahr]], where the glass is thickest, and are said to be very old.
