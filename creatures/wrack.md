## What

Wrack is what the sea throws back onto the shingle at [[wrackhead]]: weed, spars, a boot. In
[[kell]] the sea throws back the ones who went down and stayed. Each comes up again where it
fell, holding what it held, standing on the ground the ledger last counted for it.

Yours come up too. Every time you went down and did not come back, something with your name
and your gear stayed on the floor where you fell, and it holds that ground now. It does not
know you. It is old ground, and it is yours, and it is still not good.
