## Who

Since [[the-ebb]], everybody on the coast who owns a knife and a rope has been down the
[[quaysteps]] at least once. Most come up with a bollard-ring and a story. Some do not come
up, and of those, some are not dead: they have found a dry house in the [[lowstreets]] and
are living off what they find and what they take from the people who come after.

## Voice

They do not talk to you. They talk about you, to each other, while you can hear.

## In the world

- The [[greycloaks]] tithe what the pickers bring up. The pickers who stay down have stopped
  paying.
