## Who

Since [[the-ebb]] everybody on the coast with a knife and a rope has been down the
[[quaysteps]]. Most come up. Some found a dry room below and stayed, and live off what they
find and what they take from whoever comes after.

## Voice

They do not talk to you. They talk about you, to each other, while you can hear.
