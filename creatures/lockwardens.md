## What

Every strongroom door had two locks and a seal on each, and a man to each lock. They were chosen
for not being able to count, so that they could not be bribed with a number. They stand at the
doors in [[strongrooms]] in iron gone white with salt, and when somebody comes through who is not
in the ledger, they put their shoulders down and go through them.
