## What

[[vorr]] keeps what the sea takes, and the sea took [[kell]] whole. The people are still
in it. At low water they stand in their doorways in the [[lowstreets]] with the silt to their
knees, as still as the bollards. When the flood comes back they wake and walk with it,
down toward the hall, as if somebody had called them home.

## Voice

None. Their mouths move. Brother [[ruun]] says they are saying their names, so that the sea
does not forget them, and that it never has.

## In the world

- The coast folk do not call them dead. They call them kept, and pay the salt-pile, and do
  not ask.
