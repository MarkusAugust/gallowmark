## What

Grey, heavy, and bred for one thing: to chew. A knotrat's teeth grow a finger a month and it must
gnaw or die. [[the-kneeling]] feed them on hemp. Loosed under a gallows, a nest of them will have
the rope down by dawn and the beam by the end of the week.

## In the world

- [[the-ninth-rope]] keep terriers at every gallows of [[hangmoot]], and the terriers are the
  best-fed dogs in the Mark.
