## What

The [[ossar]] did not only eat men. They bred them: for size, for silence, for blindness, so that
servants in the temple would never see what they served. The Blindkin are the result. Pale,
smooth-faced where the eyes should be, hairless, quick in the dark and slow in the light.

## Now

They never left. In the deep of [[the-black-step]] and under the ruins of the [[glassands]] they
still sweep the floors, still tend [[the-stonesleepers]], still wait. [[qarr-dhuun]] diggers meet
them sometimes, and do not come up.
