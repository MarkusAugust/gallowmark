## What

Every pact with [[the-unborn]] is paid in three coins, in order: body, memory, soul. The Paid
have given the third. They look like people, mostly. They do not eat, sleep or remember. They
do what something below tells them, slowly and without stopping.

## In the world

- Sorcerer-kings of the [[glassands]] keep them as guards. [[qeth-ummar]] has a hall full.
- [[the-kneeling]] call them *the finished*, and envy them.
- Hangmen burn them, because a rope does not hold what has no breath to stop.
