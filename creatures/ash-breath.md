## What

The crack in the [[the-underhollow]] breathes. On the in-breath the smoke goes down, and the ash
with it. On the out-breath something comes up that is not smoke: heat with a shape to it, closer
kin to the [[seepers]] than anything else, but it has not had time to cool. It burns what it
touches, and it wants to touch something before the crack breathes in again.

## In the world

- [[mardra]] says to count the breaths and walk between them. She does not say what happens to
  those who lose count.
