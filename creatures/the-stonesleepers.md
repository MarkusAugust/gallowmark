## What

Not every [[ossar]] went under the earth with their masters. Some lay down in the stone of their
temples, in niches cut to fit, and slept. The knot holds them too, but less. They lie under
[[the-black-step]] and the black foundations of the [[glassands]], tended by [[the-blindkin]].

## The waking

Since [[brandfell]] split, the niches have begun to empty. One in the time of [[the-burning-of-ossery]].
Three in the last generation. [[the-kneeling]] have a word for each one that wakes. The
building on [[smokemouth]] is in their pattern.
