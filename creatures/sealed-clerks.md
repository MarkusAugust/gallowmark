## What

The doors held. The clerks inside did not, but they did not stop working either. They sit at
their desks in the [[strongrooms]] with their pens wet with brine, and when you come in they look
up, and write. What they write down sticks: every line is a notch, as with the [[notchers]] in
the vaults above, only quicker, because the clerks are better at it.

## In the world

- The [[firstcloaks]] say the clerks of Kell were the first of their own kind. The Firstcloaks
  are polite about it.
