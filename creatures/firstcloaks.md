## What

The [[greycloaks]] began as the men who guarded the drowned quays of [[kell]] in
[[the-ashfall]]. Not all of them were relieved. On the [[quaysteps]] they still stand their
posts, cloaked in grey silt, and they still tithe.

## Voice

"Pardon. A tenth." Pay, and they stand aside. Refuse, and the hook comes out.

## In the world

- The fort at [[wrackhead]] keeps no list of the men it lost in the grey. [[tolm]] has asked
  for one, politely, twice.
