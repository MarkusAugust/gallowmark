## What

Every door in the [[lowstreets]] is barred, and behind most of them somebody is still
leaning on it. The Barred drowned with their shoulders to the wood. They do not come out.
They do not want you to go in. Open a door anyway and they let go of it, all at once, and
whatever they were keeping out comes past them.

## In the world

- The coast folk say [[vorr]] keeps what the sea takes. The Barred are what he took. What is
  behind the doors he did not.
