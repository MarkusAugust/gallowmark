## What

Kell had a watch before the [[greycloaks]] had a cloak. It stood on the quays the night
[[sarn]] came, and nobody it stopped was the one it should have stopped. The sea kept it
where it stood. The Quaywatch are [[the-kept]] who were on duty: mailed, armed, in rows, and
still guarding the steps against the thief they missed.

## Voice

"Who goes?" Once. Then the spear.

## In the world

- The Greycloaks began as the men who guarded the drowned quays. Nobody in the order says
  whom they relieved.
