## What

The elders of [[bonereach]] do not ask a returning warrior where he has been. Nobody asked
the drowned of the [[lowstreets]] either. They went under with their doors barred, and some
of them did not stay under. Forty years in the dark with nothing to eat but the others.
They are thin. They are quick. They come for the hungry first.

## In the world

- The pickers say they have met people they knew, who went down too often and too hungry.
