## What

The elders of [[bonereach]] do not ask a returning warrior where he has been. Nobody asked
the people of the [[lowstreets]] either. They lived through [[the-ashfall]] in barred houses
with the sea above them and the grey inside, for a generation, and when it lifted they were
still alive. They are thin. They are quick. They come for the hungry first.

## In the world

- Anyone who means it too often in the grey becomes one of them. The pickers say they have
  met people they knew.
