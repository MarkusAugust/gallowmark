## What

When a debt could not be paid in coin the clerks of Kell took something else, and wrote it in
the ledger, and kept it in the [[strongrooms]] until the debt was paid. The debts were never paid.
They are thin and grey and still tied with the clerks' tape at the wrist, and they do not fight
so much as hold on, the way the [[the-drawn]] hold on, and pull you to the desk to be written down.
