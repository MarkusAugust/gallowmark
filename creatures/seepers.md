## What

Not [[the-unborn]] themselves. Something that comes off them, the way steam comes off a pot. It
rises through the warm cracks that [[brandfell]] opened, slow as water, and somewhere between
the deep and the light it takes a shape: a hand, a mouth, something the size of a dog with too
many joints. The knocking in the deepest shafts of [[irondeep]] is them.

## In the world

- Fire drives them back. Cold stops them. They are coming up further every year.
