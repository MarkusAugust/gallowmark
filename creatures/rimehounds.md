## What

Rimehounds are wolves the way a glacier is a stream. White, silent, the size of a horse, they
den in the far ice and come south once a year, on the longest night, in packs of a dozen. What
is outside a wall that night is theirs. The [[rimeclans]] bring the herds in, bar the doors, and
sing loud enough not to hear.

## In the world

- A clan-chief's cloak is a Rimehound hide, or it is not a chief's cloak. [[hrok]] has two.
- Since [[brandfell]] split, the packs come further south each winter, and some have been seen
  in [[bonereach]].
