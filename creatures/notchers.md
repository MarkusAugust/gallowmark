## What

Everything owed in the Mark is owed to [[hask]]'s rope, and the [[brinevaults]] are where
Kell kept its accounts. The Notchers keep them still. They do not touch you. They watch, and
cut a notch in the salt for every moment you are in their sight, and they cut slowly, and
there are nine gallows on [[hangmoot]].

At the ninth notch the debt is called. A rope comes down out of the dark, and you are one of
[[the-drawn]].
