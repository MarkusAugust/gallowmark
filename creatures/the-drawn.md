## What

The hangmen of [[hangmoot]] say a rope that went slack forty-one years ago is being drawn
tight. In the [[brinevaults]] you can see what is on the end of it. The Drawn are the
hanged of Kell, each with a rope at the neck running off into the dark toward the hall,
and each moving along it, slowly, whether it wants to or not.

They do not attack. They hold on. Whatever a Drawn takes hold of goes where the rope goes.

## In the world

- Every debt in the Mark is paid at [[hask]]'s rope. The Drawn are the ones still paying.
