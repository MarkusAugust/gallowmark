## What

The [[bricked-rooms]] were shut from the inside, and the packing filled every crack. Some of them
are open now. Not broken in: pushed out, a brick at a time, from the back. The ones who did it
are grey with mortar and still have the silver in their hands. They do not run. They wait in the
next room with their backs to the wall, the way they waited the first time, and when you come in
they close the door behind you.
