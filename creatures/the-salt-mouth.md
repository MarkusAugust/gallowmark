## What

Under the floor of [[the-iron-hall]] there is a hole in the world, and the sea is going out
through it. That is [[the-ebb]], seen from below. In the hole is the thing [[sarn]] calls
the one who keeps: a mouth of salt and old iron, wide as the hall, that does not move.
It pulls. Water, silt, [[the-kept]], [[the-drawn]] on their ropes: all of drowned [[kell]]
runs toward it, a little more every month.

## In the world

- The coast folk say it is how [[vorr]] keeps his mouth. The river-priests do not name it,
  which is the strongest thing they have ever said about anything.
- Nobody knows whether it can be killed. The way out of the hall does not need it to be.
