## What

Since [[the-ebb]], on still mornings, the fishers of [[drownhaven]] and [[ebbmouth]] have seen a
black ridge in the shallows that was not there before: barnacled, ridged, a mile long. It is
there one day and gone the next, somewhere else. Twice it has moved while boats were near it, and
the boats did not come back.

## What the coast says

That [[vorr]] keeps what the sea takes, and he took this a long time ago and kept it down. That
the sea going out is letting it up. That it is waiting for the water to go out far enough.
