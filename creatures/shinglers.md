## What

Crabs. Only crabs, the coast folk say, and then they show you the scar. The shinglers of
[[quaysteps]] have eaten well for forty years and grown to the width of a shield, with backs
gone white in the dark and one claw always bigger than the other. They come out at low
water, when the steps are theirs, and go back under when the flood comes.

## In the world

- Pickers from [[wrackhead]] eat them. The shinglers return the favour.
