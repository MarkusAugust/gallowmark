## What

A hole in a wall, at low water. A hole with teeth, at the flood. Gapes are long grey bodies
that live in the cracks of drowned [[kell]] and are nothing but a ring of teeth where they
meet the air. They do not chase. They wait for the water to bring them something, and since
[[the-ebb]] the water brings them people.

## In the world

- The coast folk say every gape is a little of [[vorr]], and that he has been opening his
  mouth in a great many places.
