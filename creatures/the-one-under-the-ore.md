## What it is

The miners of [[irondeep]] followed the vein for a hundred years and never asked why it curved.
It curves round a shape, upright, arms at its sides, the way the [[ossar]] are drawn on the walls
of their temples. Since [[brandfell]] the ore around it has gone soft. It has moved one arm.
