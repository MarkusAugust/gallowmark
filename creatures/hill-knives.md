## What

The streets that had nothing went up the hill to the streets that had something. The water
caught them on the way, in the lanes, knife in hand. They are thin and quick and they come in
packs, the way they came that night, and they still go for the door with the bar behind it. The
biggest of them carried the beams and the hammers. The pickers call those Doorbreakers, and run.

## In the world

- Every barred door in the [[lowstreets]] has scratches on the outside, low down. These are the
  hands that made them.
